Yuno's Privacy Policy explains what personal data Yuno collects, why it is collected, and how it is used, shared, and protected across the Yuno platform, Dashboard, and this documentation site.

Read the full, current policy at [y.uno/en/privacy](https://www.y.uno/en/privacy). For how Yuno handles payment and cardholder data specifically, see [PCI Compliance](/docs/security-and-compliance/pci-compliance) and [Security & Compliance](/docs/security-and-compliance/3d-secure) elsewhere in this documentation.

Questions about a specific data request or this policy can be sent to [support@y.uno](mailto:support@y.uno).
