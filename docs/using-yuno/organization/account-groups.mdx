title: "Account groups"
description: "Bundle accounts into groups so access can be granted to many accounts in a single action"
---

An **account group** bundles accounts together so you can grant access to all of them at once. Give someone a role on the group and they get that role on every account in it, including accounts added to the group later.

Open it from **Organization → Account groups**.

<Note>
**When you will not see this tab**

The tab is hidden if your organization has only one account, and it is absent if you do not have permission to view groups.
</Note>

## Groups are organization-wide

A group is not a personal shortcut. Once created it is visible to everyone in your organization who can view groups, and anyone with the right permission can add accounts to it or grant access on it. Name groups so they make sense to colleagues, not just to you.

The list shows each group's name, how many accounts it holds, how many members can reach it, and when it last changed. A notice at the top tells you how many accounts are not in any group yet.

## Creating a group

Select **Create new group** and give it a name, for example `Latam Mercosur`. You can add accounts straight away or leave it empty and add them later.

You can also nest groups: the groups you pick under **Sub-groups included** go inside the new group. A group that already sits somewhere else moves, so a group has one parent at a time.

<Note>
**Group names are not unique**

Yuno allows two groups to carry the same name. Nothing stops you creating a second `Latam`, and the list will show both, so use names your team can tell apart.
</Note>

## Changing a group

From the group's row menu you can open **Group details**, **Rename** it, **Add sub-group**, remove it from its parent, or **Delete** it.

Inside group details you can add accounts and members, and remove an account with **Remove from group**. Removing an account does not delete the account — it just stops belonging to that group, and anyone who reached it only through that group loses that route to it.

## Rules that will stop you

Two rules protect groups from being left in a broken state. Both are enforced, not warnings.

* **A group must keep at least one account.** Removing the last one is refused with *"This group needs at least one account"*. Add another account first, then remove the one you wanted to.
* **A group that still holds accounts or sub-groups cannot be deleted.** The delete dialog tells you what to clear first — *"Move its accounts out before deleting this group"*, or *"Delete or move its sub-groups before deleting this group"* — and offers no way to continue.

Together these mean a group holding exactly one account cannot be emptied by removing that account. Move the account to another group instead, using **Add to group** on the [Accounts](/docs/using-yuno/organization/accounts) tab, which moves it rather than copying it. The group is then empty and can be deleted.

## What deleting a group does to access

Deleting a group changes how accounts are organized, not who can reach them. Nobody is locked out of an account because the group around it was deleted.
