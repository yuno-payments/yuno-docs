**Organization** is where you manage everything that belongs to your company rather than to a single account: how your accounts are grouped, who can reach them, what those people can do, and how they sign in.

You open it from **Organization** in the dashboard sidebar.

<Note>
**This replaced the old profile menu items**

Account management, Team and roles, and the organization Security settings used to live behind your profile image. They are now tabs inside Organization. Your personal profile, password, and two-factor authentication stay in the profile menu, because they belong to you rather than to the organization.
</Note>

## The five tabs

| Tab | What you manage there |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Account groups](/docs/using-yuno/organization/account-groups) | Bundle accounts so access can be granted to many at once |
| [Accounts](/docs/using-yuno/organization/accounts) | Create, edit, and review the accounts in your organization |
| [Team members](/docs/using-yuno/organization/team-members) | Invite people and see the access each one actually has |
| [Roles](/docs/using-yuno/organization/roles) | Define what a role can do, using default or custom roles |
| [Security](/docs/using-yuno/organization/security) | Single sign-on and the login methods your organization allows |

Not everyone sees all five. A tab you do not have permission for is **absent rather than locked**, so the section never advertises something you cannot open. **Account groups** is also hidden if your organization has only one account, since there would be nothing to group.

## How access is granted

Access in Yuno is a **grant**: a role, given at a level.

* **Organization** — the role applies to every account you have now and every account created later.
* **Group** — the role applies to every account in that group, including accounts added to it afterwards.
* **Account** — the role applies to that one account.

One person can hold several grants at once. A member with an organization-wide role and a different role on one group holds both, and the [member detail](/docs/using-yuno/organization/team-members#what-a-member-can-actually-reach) page shows the result account by account.
