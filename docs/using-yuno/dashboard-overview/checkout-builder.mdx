The Checkout Builder is a no-code tool designed to customize your checkout experience. It includes two main modules:

* **Payment method settings**: Enable payment methods with a published route to show to your customers, reorder them, and define under which circumstances specific payment methods are displayed.
* **Checkout styling**: Adjust the checkout's visual elements for desktop and mobile devices, including colors, typography, and the payment method list's behavior. This ensures the checkout experience is consistent with your brand.

Any changes made using the Checkout Builder stay as drafts until you publish them: publishing payment method settings happens from **Review & publish**, and publishing style changes happens from **Save changes** in Checkout styling. Both are described below.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-list.png" alt="Payment method settings with one list of payment methods on the left, each row with a drag handle, an Edit button and a switch, the live preview on the right with the Payment and Save payment method switch, and Review & publish in the header" /></Frame>

## Advantages of using Checkout Builder

* **Customization with no code**: Enjoy the flexibility of tailoring your checkout experience without the need for complex coding. This empowers you to create a checkout process that aligns with your brand and business objectives.
* **Payment method flexibility**: Easily enable, reorder, and display the payment methods that best suit your customer base and market.
* **Intuitive user interface**: Yuno's interface makes it a breeze to configure payment methods, set display logic, and customize styling.

## Payment method settings

Enrolled and non-enrolled methods share a single list. Each row has an enable switch, a drag handle, and an **Edit** button that opens the method's own page.

* **Order.** Drag rows to reorder. The order is what your shoppers see, and it also decides which wallets become express buttons when more than three are switched on.
* **One item for new and saved.** **Card** and the direct-debit methods (**ACH**, **Bacs**, **PAD**, **SEPA**) have a single row that covers both the new-payment and the saved-method flow — the saved flow is switched on or off from the method page itself, with **Show shoppers their saved cards** or **Show shoppers their saved bank accounts** on the **General** tab. Other methods that can be saved, such as wallets, keep their own saved item in the list.
* **Saved methods at checkout.** The methods a returning shopper has saved render above the rest of the list, whatever the order set here.
* **Enable or disable.** A method still needs a published route (see [Routing](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/routing)) before it can be switched on here.

Clicking **Edit** opens the method's page with two tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | **Customization** (name, logo, and the express-button switch for wallets that support it), **Display logic**, **Payment options** for Card (installments, co-badged card network, credit-only processing), **Consent management** for direct-debit methods, **Saved cards** / **Saved bank accounts**, and **Accounts** for organizations with more than one account. |
| **Required fields** | What shoppers fill in when paying with this method. See [Required fields](#required-fields). |

For a wallet that supports it, such as Apple Pay, **Render as express button** on the **General** tab decides whether it appears as an express button above the list. Up to three wallets can be rendered as express buttons at once, in the order set on the payment method list.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-applepay-general.png" alt="Apple Pay's General tab showing Edit name and logo, the Render as express button switch turned on, Display logic, and a Manage Apple Pay on other accounts link under Accounts" /></Frame>

### Display logic

You can control when a payment method appears at checkout by setting a display rule based on the order's country, currency, amount, or integration metadata. Each method has **one rule**, written as a plain-language sentence: **If** an attribute **is** or **is not** some values, **Then** **Show** or **Hide** this method.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-method-page.png" alt="The Card method page on the General tab, with the Customization, Display logic and Payment options sections: Edit name and logo, When to show / hide this method with 1 rule, Installments, Co-badged cards default network and Credit card only processing" /></Frame>

To set it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the method's Display logic">
    Click **Edit** on the payment method, then **When to show / hide this method** under **Display logic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition">
    Click **Add condition**, pick an attribute and comparison, and fill in the values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the outcome">
    Set **Then** to **Show** or **Hide** this method. The Builder keeps a plain-language summary updated as you edit, for example *Card shows only when Country is AR, BO, AD. Hidden in all other cases.*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-display-logic.png" alt="The When to show / hide this method page for Card, with the sentence Card shows only when Country is AR, BO, AD, and a Show rule reading If Country is Argentina, Bolivia, Andorra, then Show this method" /></Frame>

* **Attributes:** Country, Currency, Amount, and Metadata. Country and Currency accept several values. Amount accepts greater than, less than, between, and equal to. Metadata takes a key and a value, compared with **is** or **is not** only, and a rule can hold several metadata conditions.
* **Combining conditions.** Attributes inside a rule are combined with AND, using **And condition**.
* **Amount needs one currency.** The Amount attribute isn't offered while two or more currencies are selected in the same rule.
* **No rule.** A method with no rule is always shown while it's switched on.
* **Required fields can have their own rule too.** Open a field under **Required fields** and click **Add condition** to show or hide it by country, currency, amount, or metadata — independent of the method's own display rule.

Use the **Country**, **Currency**, and **Amount** filters above the live preview to confirm a rule works as expected before publishing — see [Simulating conditions](#simulating-conditions).

## Required fields {#required-fields}

Required fields specify the information users must provide when using each payment method. Some payment methods require specific data to process payments, and Yuno may request it even if you didn't specify it.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-required-fields.png" alt="Required fields tab for Card, listing toggles for Cardholder name, First name, Last name, Document, Email, Phone number, and Billing address" /></Frame>

* Fields a provider makes mandatory show the provider's logo next to the switch, with a **Required by …** tooltip, and can't be switched off from the Builder.
* Each field can carry its own [display logic](#display-logic) rule: open the field and click **Add condition**.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-field-condition.png" alt="The First name required field expanded, showing an Add condition button to set its own display rule" /></Frame>

<Note>
  **Card and saved cards.** The Card page has a second block, **When this method is saved**, for the fields shoppers fill in when paying with a saved card. Tick **Apply these settings when shoppers save this method** to reuse the main configuration, or untick it to configure the saved-card fields separately. Display rules on Card required fields apply to both flows.
</Note>

### Address fields

Billing and shipping address can be requested together or on their own:

| Field | What it collects | Used for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Billing address** | The billing address. | Card verification (AVS) and integrations with providers such as Stripe and Adyen. |
| **Shipping address** | The shipping address, which can now be requested without billing address. | Physical delivery. |
| **Postal code** | The postal code on its own, independent of the other address fields. | Providers and SDK flows that only need a postal code. When postal code is enabled and the other address fields are disabled, the backend allows null values for every address field except postal code. If both are enabled, address fields take priority over postal code. |

When both **Billing address** and **Shipping address** are enabled, the Web SDK renders the shipping form, followed by a **"Billing address is the same as shipping address"** checkbox; this checkbox is a Web SDK UX element, not something you configure in the Builder, and the billing form only appears when it's unchecked.

<Note>
  **SDK version requirement**: Address fields are only available in **Web SDK v1.7 or later**. Merchants on earlier SDK versions won't see the address forms even if they're enabled here.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Backward compatibility:** Existing checkouts that only had billing address enabled keep working exactly as before. **Configure during low-traffic periods:** publishing changes to required fields affects every subsequent checkout session immediately, so an in-flight session may briefly see an inconsistent form during the propagation window.
</Note>

In the Required fields, you can also configure whether to request the CVV for every transaction from an enrolled card, or only on the customer's first payment.

### Document types

**Document** is the one setting in this version that's global instead of per-method: opening it shows an **Add condition** button for a per-method rule, plus a **General settings → Document types** link that opens a modal applying to every payment method in the checkout.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-document-types.png" alt="The General settings modal listing Allowed Document Types per country, with plus and minus controls to enable or disable document types such as Tazkira, Passport, and national ID cards" /></Frame>

Use the country search and the **+** / **–** controls next to each document type to control which document types are required in checkout forms, per country where you operate.

## Authorization text {#authorization-text}

Direct-debit payment methods (ACH, SEPA Direct Debit, Bacs, PAD, and iDEAL when the account is saved) require the shopper to see mandate/authorization copy before completing the payment. The **Consent management** section on a direct-debit method's **General** tab controls whether Yuno renders that copy for you or leaves it to your own checkout implementation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the method">
    Click **Edit** on a direct-debit payment method (for example, **BACS Direct Debit**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Consent management">
    On the **General** tab, find the **Consent management** section and set **Show authorization text (rendered by Yuno)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This setting is **per payment method**, not global. Repeat the configuration for every direct-debit payment method you enable.
</Note>

| Setting | Description | What the customer sees in the SDK |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Show authorization text (rendered by Yuno): ON** | Yuno renders the scheme's authorization/mandate copy for that payment method. | The checkout displays Yuno's authorization text below the bank fields. See [Direct-debit consent](/docs/sdks/resources/references/web#direct-debit-consent) for the full SDK behavior. |
| **Show authorization text (rendered by Yuno): OFF** | For merchants who render their own compliant authorization instead of Yuno's. | No authorization text is rendered by the checkout; you're responsible for displaying compliant mandate copy elsewhere, and for passing the mandate details to Yuno through `payment_method.detail.bank_transfer.mandate` on [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment). |

The live preview shows the exact authorization copy as you toggle the setting, so you can review it before publishing. This toggle choice is recorded in the audit trail, and it stays **locked**, with an explanatory tooltip, while the payment method's connection setup is incomplete — the authorization text only ever renders with real business/creditor values.

## Edit name and logo

The name and logo settings control how a payment method appears during checkout. This option is unavailable for enrolled payment methods.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customization">
    Click **Edit** on any non-enrolled payment method, then **Edit name and logo** under **Customization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to custom settings">
    Select **Use custom settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Enter a name, and optionally a description (up to 150 characters) and a logo URL. The URL must start with `https://`, and the image must be a square JPG or PNG, 100x100 pixels, up to 10 KB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Go to [Review & publish](#review-publish) for the change to take effect in the live checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-name-logo.png" alt="Edit name and logo panel with Use custom settings selected, showing Name, Description and Logo URL fields" /></Frame>

## Review & publish

Nothing goes live until you confirm it from **Review & publish**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit freely">
    While you edit, the header shows **Unsaved changes**. Changes are kept in the Builder, and the live checkout is untouched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Click **Review & publish**. The dialog lists every pending change, grouped by **Payment methods**, **Method settings**, **Required fields**, and **Styling**, with the value before and after. Changes that apply to the saved-method flow are marked **When saving**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish N changes**. The header switches to **Published**, and these changes go live for every session using this checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-review-publish.png" alt="The Review changes before publishing dialog listing a Card display rule change for both the payment and the saved flow, grouped under Payment methods, with Keep editing and Publish 2 changes buttons" /></Frame>

Leaving the Builder with unsaved changes asks you to confirm, because unpublished changes are discarded.

## Checkout styling

Match your brand's look and feel using the **Styling** tab. Changes are reflected in the end-user checkout immediately after you click **Save changes**, and the live preview updates as you edit, before you save.

### Payment method list style

Controls how the payment method list behaves for your customers.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-styling-list-style.png" alt="Payment method list style section with toggles for Unfolded display, Preselected payment method, Condensed checkout view and Edit payment method list, plus Horizontal or Vertical Express button stacking" /></Frame>

| Setting | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Unfolded display** | Shows the card form expanded in place, integrated into the payment methods list, instead of opening it in a separate modal. |
| **Preselected payment method** | Displays a preselected payment method when the checkout loads, instead of showing the list with nothing selected. |
| **Condensed checkout view** | Reduces visual clutter by showing just the preselected payment method; additional options remain available under **View more**. |
| **Edit payment method list** | Lets customers view their own enrolled payment methods and delete them directly from the list. |
| **Express button stacking** | **Horizontal** sits multiple express buttons side by side on one line; **Vertical** stacks them in a full-width column, one per line. |

### Forms collect data

Controls the primary button and the Yuno-rendered form chrome.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-styling-forms-collect-data.png" alt="Forms collect data section with a Button text dropdown set to Pay, a Yuno Pay button toggle marked Universal SDK only, and a Display Powered by Yuno tag toggle" /></Frame>

| Setting | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Button text** | Customizes the primary button's label to align with your checkout flow, for example service bookings, subscriptions, or donations. |
| **Yuno Pay button** (Universal SDK only) | When on, Yuno renders a default Pay button that stays in sync with form validation and manages the loading state during payment. Turn it off if you render your own Pay button and handle loading and transition states yourself. |
| **Display 'Powered by Yuno' tag** | Shows or hides the "Powered by Yuno" tag at the bottom of the payment form. Turning it off also hides the Privacy Policy link next to it. |

### Typography

Choose a font from Yuno's list to apply to the entire checkout design.

### Colors

Select and customize the color palette that defines the checkout's visual appearance.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-styling-colors.png" alt="Colors section with a Use system settings dark mode toggle, and color pickers for Accent color, Background color, Field background color, Secondary background color, Primary text color and Secondary text color" /></Frame>

| Customizable element | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Use system settings (dark mode)** | Automatically switches between light and dark mode according to the shopper's device settings, using Yuno's built-in dark theme for dark surfaces while keeping your **Accent color**, **Primary button text color**, and **Typography** where supported. Enabling it disables manual color editing below. |
| **Accent color** | Highlights interactive elements: the primary button's background, the secondary button's border, and checkbox colors. |
| **Background color** | The background applied across all checkout forms. |
| **Field background color** | The background of input fields. |
| **Secondary background color** | A secondary surface color used across the checkout. |
| **Primary text color** | Titles, subtitles, input values, dropdown values, text on secondary buttons, and checkbox text. |
| **Secondary text color** | Field descriptions, placeholder text, and help text. |
| **Primary button text color** | The text color on the primary button. |

### Form styles

Customize the detailed design of the payment form — borders, typography, colors, and corner radius — starting with the **Header**, which controls how your logo and checkout title appear (logo border, logo border color, logo corner radius, font size, and font weight).

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-styling-form-styles.png" alt="Form styles section expanded to the Header subsection, with controls for Logo border, Logo border color, Logo corner radius, Font size and Font weight" /></Frame>

<Note>
  Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the Styling tab to publish style changes, or **Restore default styles** to revert to Yuno's defaults.
</Note>

## Real-time checkout preview

The Checkout Builder includes a live preview panel on the right side of the screen, available in both **Payment method settings** and **Styling settings**. It reflects your current configuration as you make changes, without requiring you to publish or save first.

### What the preview shows

- The payment methods currently enabled and their display order
- Express payment buttons when active
- The card form with all configured required fields
- Color, typography, and styling changes as you adjust them

### Payment or Save payment method

A switch above the preview chooses which flow to display:

- **Payment** shows the checkout a shopper sees when paying: express buttons, saved methods, and the rest of the list, in the order you set.
- **Save payment method** shows the enrollment checkout, titled **Save a payment method**: only the methods a shopper can save, with a **Save payment method** button instead of **Pay**.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-preview-save.png" alt="The live preview on Save payment method, showing the Save a payment method checkout with Card, PayPal, iDeal, NuPay and PayPal Enrollment and a Save payment method button" /></Frame>

Use it to check both flows while you edit a method, in particular the **When this method is saved** fields on Card and the **Show shoppers their saved cards** / **Show shoppers their saved bank accounts** switches.

### Interactive fields

You can interact with the form fields directly in the preview panel to test field validation and error messages — for example, entering an invalid CVV triggers the error copy in real time.

<Note>
The preview supports interactive field testing up to the **Pay** button. It does not simulate a complete end-to-end payment flow. To test a full transaction, use the SDK in a [test environment](https://docs.y.uno/docs/yuno-testing-gateway).
</Note>

### Simulating conditions {#simulating-conditions}

Use the **Country**, **Currency**, and **Amount** filters above the preview panel — available in both tabs — to simulate different order contexts. This lets you verify that your [display logic](#display-logic) works as expected before publishing: for example, selecting a country your rule doesn't match hides the method, and the preview shows **Your checkout is empty** if nothing else is enabled for that context.
