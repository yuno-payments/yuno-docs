<Note>
  **How to tell which version you have.** This page describes the new **Payment method settings** design, rolling out account by account: one list of payment methods, an **Edit** button per method, and a **Review & publish** button in the header. If your dashboard instead shows two lists (**Enrolled** and **Non-enrolled payment methods**), a **three dots (⋯)** menu per row, and a **Publish settings** button, you're still on the previous version — use the [Checkout Builder](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder) page instead.

  **Checkout styling** and the real-time preview work the same regardless of which version you have, and are described on the [Checkout Builder](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder#checkout-styling) page — except for the **Payment** / **Save payment method** preview switch, which is new in this version and described below.
</Note>

## Payment method settings

Enrolled and non-enrolled methods share a single list. Each row has an enable switch, a drag handle, and an **Edit** button that opens the method's own page.

* **Order.** Drag rows to reorder. The order is what your shoppers see, and it also decides which wallets become express buttons when more than three are switched on.
* **One item for new and saved.** **Card** and the direct-debit methods (**ACH**, **Bacs**, **PAD**, **SEPA**) have a single row that covers both the new-payment and the saved-method flow — the saved flow is switched on or off from the method page itself, with **Show shoppers their saved cards** or **Show shoppers their saved bank accounts** on the **General** tab. Other methods that can be saved, such as wallets, keep their own saved item in the list.
* **Saved methods at checkout.** The methods a returning shopper has saved render above the rest of the list, whatever the order set here.
* **Enable or disable.** A method still needs a published route (see [Routing](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/routing)) before it can be switched on here.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-list.png" alt="Payment method settings with one list of payment methods on the left, each row with a drag handle, an Edit button and a switch, the live preview on the right with the Payment and Save payment method switch, and Review & publish in the header" /></Frame>

Clicking **Edit** opens the method's page with two tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | **Customization** (name, logo, and the express-button switch for wallets that support it), **Display logic**, **Payment options** for Card (installments, co-badged card network, credit-only processing), **Consent management** for direct-debit methods, **Saved cards** / **Saved bank accounts**, and **Accounts** for organizations with more than one account. |
| **Required fields** | What shoppers fill in when paying with this method. See [Required fields](#required-fields). |

For a wallet that supports it, such as Apple Pay, **Render as express button** on the **General** tab decides whether it appears as an express button above the list. Up to three wallets can be rendered as express buttons at once, in the order set on the payment method list.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-applepay-general.png" alt="Apple Pay's General tab showing Edit name and logo, the Render as express button switch turned on, Display logic, and a Manage Apple Pay on other accounts link under Accounts" /></Frame>

### Display logic

You can control when a payment method appears at checkout by setting a display rule based on the order's country, currency, amount, or integration metadata. Each method has **one rule**, written as a plain-language sentence: **If** an attribute **is** or **is not** some values, **Then** **Show** or **Hide** this method.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-method-page.png" alt="The Card method page on the General tab, with the Customization, Display logic and Payment options sections: Edit name and logo, When to show / hide this method with 1 rule, Installments, Co-badged cards default network and Credit card only processing" /></Frame>

To set it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the method's Display logic">
    Click **Edit** on the payment method, then **When to show / hide this method** under **Display logic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition">
    Click **Add condition**, pick an attribute and comparison, and fill in the values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the outcome">
    Set **Then** to **Show** or **Hide** this method. The Builder keeps a plain-language summary updated as you edit, for example *Card shows only when Country is AR, BO, AD. Hidden in all other cases.*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-display-logic.png" alt="The When to show / hide this method page for Card, with the sentence Card shows only when Country is AR, BO, AD, and a Show rule reading If Country is Argentina, Bolivia, Andorra, then Show this method" /></Frame>

* **Attributes:** Country, Currency, Amount, and Metadata. Country and Currency accept several values. Amount accepts greater than, less than, between, and equal to. Metadata takes a key and a value, compared with **is** or **is not** only, and a rule can hold several metadata conditions.
* **Combining conditions.** Attributes inside a rule are combined with AND, using **And condition**.
* **Amount needs one currency.** The Amount attribute isn't offered while two or more currencies are selected in the same rule.
* **No rule.** A method with no rule is always shown while it's switched on.
* **Required fields can have their own rule too.** Open a field under **Required fields** and click **Add condition** to show or hide it by country, currency, amount, or metadata — independent of the method's own display rule.

Use the **Country**, **Currency**, and **Amount** filters above the live preview to confirm a rule works as expected before publishing — see [Simulating conditions](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder#simulating-conditions).

Your existing conditions are carried over. Values stored as *equal* or *not equal* read as **is** and **is not**.

<Note>
  Accounts that have **more than one active condition set on the same payment method** stay on the previous version for now, with their configurations untouched.

  The same applies to accounts whose conditions compare **metadata with greater than or less than**: this version only offers **is** and **is not** for metadata, so those rules cannot be edited here. They stay on the previous version until the rules are rewritten.
</Note>

## Required fields {#required-fields}

Required fields specify the information users must provide when using each payment method. Some payment methods require specific data to process payments, and Yuno may request it even if you didn't specify it.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-required-fields.png" alt="Required fields tab for Card, listing toggles for Cardholder name, First name, Last name, Document, Email, Phone number, and Billing address" /></Frame>

* Fields a provider makes mandatory show the provider's logo next to the switch, with a **Required by …** tooltip, and can't be switched off from the Builder.
* Each field can carry its own [display logic](#display-logic) rule: open the field and click **Add condition**.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-field-condition.png" alt="The First name required field expanded, showing an Add condition button to set its own display rule" /></Frame>

<Note>
  **Card and saved cards.** The Card page has a second block, **When this method is saved**, for the fields shoppers fill in when paying with a saved card. Tick **Apply these settings when shoppers save this method** to reuse the main configuration, or untick it to configure the saved-card fields separately. Display rules on Card required fields apply to both flows.
</Note>

Billing and shipping address can now be requested together or on their own: shipping address can be requested without billing address, which wasn't possible in the previous version. See [Required fields](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder#required-fields) on the main page for the SDK version requirement and the rest of the address-field behavior, which is unchanged.

### Document types

**Document** is the one setting in this version that's global instead of per-method: opening it shows an **Add condition** button for a per-method rule, plus a **General settings → Document types** link that opens a modal applying to every payment method in the checkout.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-document-types.png" alt="The General settings modal listing Allowed Document Types per country, with plus and minus controls to enable or disable document types such as Tazkira, Passport, and national ID cards" /></Frame>

Use the country search and the **+** / **–** controls next to each document type to control which document types are required in checkout forms, per country where you operate. Previously, document type options were reached from a drawer on each payment method.

## Authorization text {#authorization-text}

The **Consent management** section on a direct-debit method's **General** tab replaces the old **Set authorization text** item in the three-dots menu. Click **Edit** on a direct-debit payment method (for example, **BACS Direct Debit**), then set **Show authorization text (rendered by Yuno)** under **Consent management**. Everything else about this setting, including the available configuration and the audit-trail note, is unchanged — see [Authorization text](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder#authorization-text) on the main page.

## Edit name and logo

The name and logo settings control how a payment method appears during checkout. This option is unavailable for enrolled payment methods.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customization">
    Click **Edit** on any non-enrolled payment method, then **Edit name and logo** under **Customization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to custom settings">
    Select **Use custom settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Enter a name, and optionally a description (up to 150 characters) and a logo URL. The URL must start with `https://`, and the image must be a square JPG or PNG, 100x100 pixels, up to 10 KB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Go to [Review & publish](#review-publish) for the change to take effect in the live checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-name-logo.png" alt="Edit name and logo panel with Use custom settings selected, showing Name, Description and Logo URL fields" /></Frame>

This is now an inline panel on the method's page instead of a separate dialog, and **Copy to other accounts** moved to **Manage [method] on other accounts** on the **General** tab.

## Review & publish

**Publish settings** is replaced by **Review & publish**. Nothing goes live until you confirm from the review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit freely">
    While you edit, the header shows **Unsaved changes**. Changes are kept in the Builder, and the live checkout is untouched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Click **Review & publish**. The dialog lists every pending change, grouped by **Payment methods**, **Method settings**, **Required fields**, and **Styling**, with the value before and after. Changes that apply to the saved-method flow are marked **When saving**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish N changes**. The header switches to **Published**, and these changes go live for every session using this checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-review-publish.png" alt="The Review changes before publishing dialog listing a Card display rule change for both the payment and the saved flow, grouped under Payment methods, with Keep editing and Publish 2 changes buttons" /></Frame>

Leaving the Builder with unsaved changes asks you to confirm, because unpublished changes are discarded.

## Preview: payment or save payment method

The live preview gains a switch at the top with two options:

- **Payment** shows the checkout a shopper sees when paying: express buttons, saved methods such as a card ending in 1234, and the rest of the list, in the order you set.
- **Save payment method** shows the enrollment checkout, titled **Save a payment method**: only the methods a shopper can save, with a **Save payment method** button instead of **Pay**.

<Frame><img src="/images/reference/checkout-builder/new-settings-preview-save.png" alt="The live preview on Save payment method, showing the Save a payment method checkout with Card, PayPal, iDeal, NuPay and PayPal Enrollment and a Save payment method button" /></Frame>

Use it to check both flows while you edit a method, in particular the **When this method is saved** fields on Card and the **Show shoppers their saved cards** and **Show shoppers their saved bank accounts** switches. The Country, Currency and Amount filters, and the interactive form, work in both — see [Real-time checkout preview](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/checkout-builder#real-time-checkout-preview) on the main page.

## A short tour the first time you open it

The first time you open the new version, the Builder walks you through the list, a method page, required fields, the preview and **Review & publish**. The tour runs on demo data: nothing is published and your live checkout stays as it is. You can skip it from the first step.

## What did not change

- **Styling settings**, including payment method list style, dark mode and express button stacking.
- **The live preview**, its interactive form and the Country, Currency and Amount filters, apart from the new **Payment** / **Save payment method** switch.
- **Custom checkouts** and the checkout selector above the preview.
- **Your SDK integration.** The new section writes the same configuration the SDK already reads. No code change is needed on your side.
