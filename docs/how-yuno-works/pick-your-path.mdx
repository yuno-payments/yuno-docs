title: "Pick your integration path"
description: "Choose between SDK checkout, Direct API, or connecting providers based on your use case"
---

Choose the integration approach that fits your use case. All paths lead to accepting payments through Yuno's orchestration platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SDK checkout" icon="code" href="/docs/sdks/overview/quickstart">
    **Best for:** Fast integration with pre-built UI

    Use our Web, iOS, or Android SDKs. Yuno handles the checkout form, payment methods display, and secure card capture. You focus on your backend and business logic.

    Start with [SDK Quickstart →](/docs/sdks/overview/quickstart)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Direct API" icon="server" href="/docs/direct-integration-use-cases/direct-flow">
    **Best for:** Full control over UI and flow

    Build your own payment form and call Yuno APIs directly. Requires PCI compliance for card handling. Use the [Testing Gateway](/docs/direct-integration-use-cases/yuno-testing-gateway) to validate your integration.

    Start with [Direct Flow →](/docs/direct-integration-use-cases/direct-flow)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Payment Links" icon="link" href="/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/payment-links">
    **Best for:** No-code payment collection

    Create payment links from the Dashboard and share them with customers. No integration required.

    Learn about [Payment Links →](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/payment-links)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Plugins" icon="puzzle-piece" href="/docs/plugins/vtex/index">
    **Best for:** E-commerce platforms

    Pre-built integrations for VTEX, WooCommerce, and other platforms.

    See [Plugins →](/docs/plugins/vtex/index)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Next steps

Before you integrate, complete the account setup:

1. **[Set up your account](/docs/how-yuno-works/step-1-set-up-your-account)** — Create your Yuno account, connect the Test Payment Gateway, and configure routing.
2. **[Get your credentials](/docs/using-yuno/settings/developers-credentials)** — Obtain your API keys from the Dashboard.
3. **[First payment](/docs/how-yuno-works/step-2-your-first-payment)** — Run the SDK sample project to process a sandbox payment.
