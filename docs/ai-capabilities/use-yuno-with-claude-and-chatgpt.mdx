Connect your Yuno account to the AI assistant you already use. Analyze your payments data, search the Yuno API documentation, and operate payments on the Yuno accounts you have access to, all in plain language. You sign in with your Yuno user, so the assistant only sees and acts on what your user and organization are allowed to.

<Note>
  If you're a developer looking to build payment flows into your own agent instead of using this ready-made connector, see [Building AI Integrations with Yuno's LLMs and MCP](/docs/ai-capabilities/building-ai-integrations-with-yunos-llms-and-mcp) and the [Remote Yuno MCP Server](/docs/ai-capabilities/remote-yuno-mcp-server) instead.
</Note>

## Accounts

Payment actions run on one of your Yuno accounts. By default, the connector uses your principal account: the one set as default for your Yuno user in the dashboard, or the first account you have access to otherwise.

* Ask **"Which Yuno accounts can I use?"** to list the accounts available to you and see which one is the default.
* Tell the assistant which account to use for a given request (for example, "use account `<account>`") to switch away from the default.
* Accounts you don't have access to are rejected: the connector can only use accounts your Yuno user is entitled to.

## Safety model

* **Analytics is read-only**: SQL statements that modify data or schema are always rejected.
* **Payment tools can write**: they can create and modify data. Depending on your Claude or ChatGPT settings, the assistant may ask for your approval before running them.
* **Production confirmation**: on production, cancellations, refunds, and deletions return a preview first and only run after the assistant confirms within 5 minutes.
* Permissions come from your Yuno user's access and organization, not from the assistant: it can only do what your account is already allowed to do.

## What you can do

The connector exposes tools across four capability groups: analytics, Yuno API documentation, accounts, and payments. The assistant chooses the right tool automatically, so you never have to call them by name, but knowing what's available helps you know what to ask.

### Analytics (read-only SQL)

| Tool | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_query` | Read | Runs read-only SQL (`SELECT`, `WITH`, `SHOW`, `DESCRIBE`, `EXPLAIN`) against your analytics data. This powers most questions: metrics, breakdowns, trends, exports. |
| `table_overview` | Read | Describes a table's columns and types and shows a few sample rows, so the assistant learns your data's shape before querying it. |
| `analyze_query` | Read | Returns the execution plan of a query without running it, so expensive queries can be sanity-checked first. |

### Yuno API documentation

| Tool | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `docs__search_yuno_api_documentation` | Read | Searches the public Yuno API documentation. |
| `docs__query_docs_filesystem_yuno_api_documentation` | Read | Reads documentation pages. |
| `docs__submit_feedback` | Write | Reports an incorrect or outdated docs page to the docs team. |

### Accounts

| Tool | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `yuno_list_accounts` | Read | Lists the Yuno accounts you can use and marks the default one. |

## Ask in plain language

* "What was my payment approval rate by payment method over the last 30 days?"
* "Show transaction volume by country for July, and flag any country that dropped more than 10% vs June."
* "What are my top 5 decline reasons this month, and which provider do they come from?"
* "Compare conversion between credit card and PIX in Brazil this quarter."
* "Describe the transactions table and show me a few sample rows."
* "Build me a weekly summary: volume, approval rate, refund rate, and average ticket."
* "How do I create a payment with 3DS?"
* "Which Yuno accounts can I use?"
* "Refund payment `<payment_id>`."
* "Create a payment link for 50 USD."

## Scoped to your account

* **Scoped to your account**: The connector authenticates with your Yuno credentials and can only see and act on the accounts your user is entitled to. See [Safety model](#safety-model) for how read-only analytics and payment writes are kept safe.
* **Query limits**: Very large result sets are truncated rather than streamed in full, and sessions are rate-limited to keep the warehouse healthy. For bulk exports, use the Yuno dashboard or API.
* **Partial outages**: If one capability (analytics, documentation, or payments) is temporarily unavailable, only its tools disappear; the rest keep working.

## Troubleshooting

**Claude says the Yuno connector is disconnected**

Go to Settings → Connectors, find Yuno, and click Reconnect, then re-authorize. If the connector doesn't appear in a chat, check that it's enabled in the chat's search & tools menu.

**ChatGPT doesn't call the Yuno app**

Invoke it explicitly by typing `@Yuno` at the start of your message, or mention "using Yuno" in the prompt. If it's still unavailable, remove and re-add the app from its directory listing.

**Queries fail with a "no database selected" error**

Ask the assistant to qualify tables with their database name (for example `analytics.transactions`) or to state which database to use. Asking "what tables are available?" first usually resolves this.

**Results look truncated**

That's expected for large outputs. Ask the assistant to aggregate (totals, top-N, grouped summaries) instead of listing raw rows, or narrow the date range.

**The assistant used the wrong account**

Ask "Which Yuno accounts can I use?" and tell it which one to use for the request.

**"Confirmation expired"**

Production confirmations last 5 minutes. Ask the assistant to repeat the action to get a fresh confirmation.

**"Connect" or "consent required" message**

The assistant is asking you to (re)authorize the Yuno connector: your session or a prior consent has expired or wasn't completed. Go through the connect step again (see Setup above) and retry your request.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do the Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok connectors do the same thing?">
    Yes. All three expose the same Yuno tools: analytics, documentation, and payments. Pick the assistant your team already uses, or connect more than one; access and permissions are identical because they're governed by your Yuno credential, not by the assistant. (Grok is coming soon.)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the connector move money or change my payment configuration?">
    It can run payment operations (create, capture, cancel, and refund payments, and manage customers, subscriptions, payment links, recipients, and installment plans) on the Yuno accounts you have access to. It cannot change your Yuno dashboard or routing configuration. Destructive actions on production require an explicit confirmation step. For programmatic payment operations from your own agent, see [Building AI Integrations with Yuno's LLMs and MCP](/docs/ai-capabilities/building-ai-integrations-with-yunos-llms-and-mcp) and the [Remote Yuno MCP Server](/docs/ai-capabilities/remote-yuno-mcp-server).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data used to train AI models?">
    Your data is transferred to the assistant only within your own conversation, subject to Anthropic's or OpenAI's data policies for your plan. Consult your workspace's AI data-handling policy; enterprise plans of both providers exclude business data from training by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Yuno to my own agent or IDE as a developer?">
    Yes. Yuno publishes an MCP server you can use from Cursor, Claude Code, or any MCP-compatible client. See the [Remote Yuno MCP Server](/docs/ai-capabilities/remote-yuno-mcp-server) and [Building AI Integrations with Yuno's LLMs and MCP](/docs/ai-capabilities/building-ai-integrations-with-yunos-llms-and-mcp).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who do I contact if something's not working?">
    Reach out to your Yuno account manager or open a ticket through the Yuno dashboard. For developer issues with the MCP server, file them on [GitHub](https://github.com/yuno-payments/yuno-mcp).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
