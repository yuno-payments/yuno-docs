title: "Samsung Pay"
description: "Introduces Samsung Pay support on Yuno through the VTEX plugin, and its availability for other integrations."
---

Samsung Pay is Samsung's digital wallet that lets customers pay using the cards stored on their Samsung devices, confirming each payment with Samsung's defence-grade security (fingerprint, PIN, or iris scan). Samsung Pay works on supported Samsung Galaxy devices and select other Android phones.

## Current availability

Samsung Pay is available on Yuno through the **VTEX plugin** using the Wallet enhanced experience. See the integration guide below for setup instructions.

<Note>
  **Using VTEX?**

  If you are a VTEX merchant, follow the [Wallet enhanced experience guide](/docs/plugins/vtex/apple-pay-google-pay-enhanced-experience) to enable Samsung Pay in your VTEX checkout. The guide covers the setup snippet, Samsung Pay installments, and the VTEX order display caveat.
</Note>

## Installments

Samsung Pay supports installment plans through the Yuno dashboard. Create plans with `payment_method_type: SAMSUNG_PAY` using the [Create Installments Plan](/reference/installments/create-installments-plan) endpoint, or from the dashboard under **Installments**.

See the [Samsung Pay installments section](/docs/plugins/vtex/apple-pay-google-pay-enhanced-experience#samsung-pay-installments) in the VTEX Wallet enhanced experience guide for details on how installments work and how to set them up.

## Non-VTEX integrations

Samsung Pay availability outside VTEX — through the Web SDK, Android SDK, or Direct API — is being evaluated. Once confirmed, integration guides for those paths will be added here.

## Supported devices

Samsung Pay is available on Samsung Galaxy devices (phones and watches) running Android 6.0 or later, and on select other Android devices. It uses either NFC for contactless payments at the terminal or MST (Magnetic Secure Transmission) on older devices for backward compatibility.
