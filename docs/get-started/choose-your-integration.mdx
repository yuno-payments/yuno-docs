Start with **Seamless SDK** if you want Yuno to display available payment methods and provide checkout components. Choose a more customized integration when your product needs it.

<Note>
Every API-backed integration needs a server. Your private secret key stays there; only the public API key belongs in a client application. Available payment methods depend on your account, country, provider connections, and published routing.
</Note>

## What do you want to build?

| Your goal | Start with | What you own | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch a configurable checkout with pre-built UI | **Seamless SDK** | Your application, backend, and checkout configuration | [Web](/docs/sdks/seamless-sdk/web-payments), [iOS](/docs/sdks/seamless-sdk/ios-payments), [Android](/docs/sdks/seamless-sdk/android-payments) |
| Design your own payment-method selector | **Lite SDK** | Method selection and checkout orchestration | [Web guide](/docs/sdks/lite-web/payment) |
| Build the whole payment interface | **Headless SDK** | UI, field validation, method-specific input, and SDK lifecycle | [Web guide](/docs/sdks/headless-web/payment) |
| Embed secure card inputs into a web form | **Secure Fields** | The surrounding form and payment flow | [Secure Fields](/docs/sdks/customization/secure-fields/index) |
| Send payment requests from your backend | **Direct API** | Payment collection, API calls, required actions, and applicable PCI obligations | [Direct flow](/docs/direct-integration-use-cases/direct-flow) |
| Share a hosted payment page | **Payment Links** | Link configuration and order fulfillment | [Payment Links](/docs/using-yuno/dashboard-overview/payment-links) |
| Add payments to an existing ecommerce store | **Store plugin** | Store setup, account configuration, and operational testing | [WooCommerce](/docs/plugins/woocommerce/index) or [VTEX](/docs/plugins/vtex/index) |

## Choose your platform

The Web SDK works with browser applications. If you use server-side rendering, initialize it only on the client; see [React and Next.js](/docs/sdks/web-frameworks/react-and-nextjs).

For mobile applications, use the platform-specific guide:

- [iOS](/docs/sdks/seamless-sdk/ios-payments) and [Android](/docs/sdks/seamless-sdk/android-payments).
- [React Native](/docs/sdks/additional-platforms/react-native/index) and [Flutter](/docs/sdks/additional-platforms/flutter).

Use the installation instructions and changelog for your chosen platform when selecting a version. A version number for one SDK does not apply to the others.

## Plan for your markets

1. Identify each transaction country, currency, and payment method you need.
2. Confirm a supported provider connection for that combination and any local payer fields.
3. Configure the connection, publish routing, and enable the method in your checkout.
4. Test the method's complete flow, including redirects, expiration, or delayed confirmation.

Consult [payment methods](/docs/basic-concepts/payment-methods), [country reference](/reference/country-reference), and [supported checkout languages](/docs/sdks/resources/languages-supported). A country or language appearing in a list does not mean every provider and payment method is enabled for your account.

## Understand the security boundary

SDK integration can keep raw card data away from your server. Your compliance obligations still depend on your implementation. Review [PCI compliance](/docs/security-and-compliance/pci-compliance) before choosing direct card-data handling.
