This page follows one sale from the amount to reconciliation, then covers voids, refunds and failures. The payment it produces is the same `payment` resource as every other Yuno channel.

## A sale, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="The amount" icon="keyboard">
    The seller keys the amount in the Yuno Tap to Pay app, or your app sends it with your order reference. Instalments, where offered, are chosen here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The acquirer is chosen" icon="route">
    Yuno applies your routing rules (merchant, market, card brand, amount, instalments) before the tap. The card data and the PIN are protected for that acquirer from the moment they are captured, which is why the choice comes first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer taps" icon="hand-pointer">
    The app shows the amount and where to tap. The customer holds their card, phone or watch on the phone until it confirms the read, usually under a second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PIN, when required" icon="lock">
    If the card, the amount or the market asks for it, a secure PIN keypad appears on the screen. The PIN is encrypted for the acquirer as it is typed; your app, your servers and Yuno never see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorisation" icon="circle-check">
    The acquirer approves or declines, usually in two to three seconds. The result appears on the phone, goes back to your app in the App to app type, and is recorded as a Yuno payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt" icon="receipt">
    By email, SMS or QR code, with your brand and the acquirer's references.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where the customer taps

| Phone | Where to tap |
| --- | --- |
| **iPhone** | The front, near the top of the screen. The seller turns the screen to face the customer |
| **Android** | Usually the back, near the camera or the middle. The position changes by model, so the tap screen shows it for the phone in use |

<Tip>
  If a tap is not read, ask the customer to hold the card still, flat, for a second, and to remove it from a thick or metal case.
</Tip>

| Field | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| `channel` | `IN_PERSON`, as for POS terminal sales; filter on `in_person.device_type` to separate phones from terminals |
| `payment_method.detail.card.entry_mode` | `CONTACTLESS` for every Tap to Pay sale |
| `payment_method.detail.card.cardholder_verification` | `NONE`, `ONLINE_PIN`, or `CONSUMER_DEVICE` when the customer verified on their own phone or watch |
| `card_data.wallet` | The wallet used, when the customer tapped a phone or watch |
| `in_person` | The phone that took the sale (`device_type`, `device_id`, `platform`), the seller, and which rule chose the acquirer |

Statuses, decline codes and webhooks are the same as for POS terminal sales: see [Payments and events](/docs/in-person/payments-and-events).

## Voids and refunds

| Action | When | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Void** | Same day, before the acquirer settles | From the app's sales list, the dashboard or the API. The sale disappears from the customer's statement |
| **Refund** | After settlement, in part or in full | From the dashboard or the API. The customer does not need to tap again |

Voids and refunds always go to the acquirer that approved the original sale, whatever your routing rules say today.

## When a sale does not complete

| What happens | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The customer removes the card too early | The app asks to tap again; nothing reaches the acquirer |
| The acquirer declines | `DECLINED`, with the reason in `response_code`. The seller asks for another card |
| No answer from the acquirer in time | The sale is reversed with the same acquirer and ends as `ERROR` / `REVERSED_BY_TIMEOUT`; no charge stands |
| The phone has no connection | The app does not start the tap. Tap to Pay needs a data connection for every sale |
| The app is closed during the sale | The payment keeps its final status in Yuno; check it by payment ID or webhook |

A declined sale is final with that acquirer: an authorisation only goes to one acquirer. Routing works across sales, to balance cost, instalment programmes and acquirer health.
