title: "Tap to Pay integration types"
description: "Run Tap to Pay with the Yuno Tap to Pay app on its own, or open it from your Android or iOS app to take the payment and return the result: what each type asks of you and how to choose."
---

The tap always happens in the Yuno Tap to Pay app, the part that is certified for card acceptance. What changes is who starts the sale: the seller, in the app itself, or your own app, which opens the Yuno Tap to Pay app and gets the result back. Both types share the same payment object, routing, webhooks, dashboard and reconciliation, and neither asks you to certify anything.

## Compare

|  | Standalone | App to app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where the sale starts** | In the Yuno Tap to Pay app | In your Android or iOS app |
| **What the seller installs** | The Yuno Tap to Pay app, with your brand | Your app and the Yuno Tap to Pay app, with your brand |
| **You build** | Nothing | One call to open the app, and handling the result |
| **Certification on your side** | None | None |
| **Best for** | Acquirers and banks offering Tap to Pay to their merchants; small sellers with no app | Platforms, delivery and field-sales apps, and merchants with their own seller app |

<Info>
  **Which one for you?** If you are an acquirer, a bank or a platform giving Tap to Pay to many sellers, start **Standalone**: your sellers download one app under your brand and take payments the same day. If your sellers already work in your app, add **App to app**: they never type the amount twice, and your app knows the result as soon as the customer taps.
</Info>

## Each type in detail

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standalone">
    The seller signs in to the Yuno Tap to Pay app, keys the amount and hands the phone to the customer. Nothing to integrate.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="You set up the app">
        Brand (logo, colours, name), enabled acquirers, routing rules, instalment plans and receipt text, from the Yuno dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sellers download it">
        From the App Store or Google Play, under your brand. Each seller is invited from the dashboard or created through the API, with their merchant and acquirer IDs.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sellers take payments">
        Amount, optional description and instalments, tap. The receipt goes by email, SMS or QR.
      </Step>
      <Step title="You see every sale">
        In the dashboard and through webhooks, with the seller and the device that took it.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **Good to know:** the app also lists the seller's sales of the day, and lets them void a sale or refund it.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="App to app">
    Your app opens the Yuno Tap to Pay app with the amount and your order reference. The customer taps, and the Yuno Tap to Pay app returns the result to your app.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Your app sends the sale">
        Amount, currency, your order reference and where to return the result.
      </Step>
      <Step title="The Yuno Tap to Pay app takes the payment">
        It chooses the acquirer with your rules, shows the tap screen and, when required, the PIN keypad.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Your app gets the result">
        Status, payment ID, authorisation code and masked card, on the return address you sent.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Yuno records the sale">
        Same payment and webhooks, with your order reference, so your backend can confirm the result server to server.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    | Direction | Fields |
    | --- | --- |
    | Your app to the Yuno Tap to Pay app | `amount`, `currency`, `merchant_order_id`, `return_url`, `installments` (optional), `customer_email` (optional, for the receipt) |
    | The Yuno Tap to Pay app to your app | `status`, `sub_status`, `payment_id`, `merchant_order_id`, `authorization_code`, `card_brand`, `card_lfd`, `entry_mode` |

    <CodeGroup>

    ```kotlin Android
    val intent = Intent("com.yuno.taptopay.action.PAY").apply {
        setPackage(TAP_TO_PAY_PACKAGE) // your branded app's package name
        putExtra("amount", "125.00")
        putExtra("currency", "MXN")
        putExtra("merchant_order_id", "ORD-48213")
    }
    taptopayLauncher.launch(intent) // result in the ActivityResult extras
    ```

    ```swift iOS
    var components = URLComponents(string: "\(tapToPayScheme)://pay")!
    components.queryItems = [
        URLQueryItem(name: "amount", value: "125.00"),
        URLQueryItem(name: "currency", value: "MXN"),
        URLQueryItem(name: "merchant_order_id", value: "ORD-48213"),
        URLQueryItem(name: "return_url", value: "myapp://taptopay/result")
    ]
    UIApplication.shared.open(components.url!)
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    Your Yuno contact gives you the package name and URL scheme of your branded app. **Always confirm the result from your backend** with the payment ID or the webhook before you release the goods: the return to your app can be lost if the seller closes it.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Want the tap to run inside your own app instead, with no second app? Talk to your Yuno contact: it is possible for some cases, and they will tell you what it involves.
</Note>
