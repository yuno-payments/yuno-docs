## Coverage

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Android** | Phones and tablets with NFC, on a supported Android version, with Google Play services. Rooted or modified devices are blocked by the app's security checks |
| **iPhone** | iPhone XS or later, on a supported iOS version, in the markets where Apple offers Tap to Pay on iPhone |
| **Markets** | Mexico, Brazil and Ecuador. More markets on demand, under the same agreement |
| **Card brands** | Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and local brands. Elo is coming soon |
| **Wallets** | Apple Pay, Google Pay and other contactless wallets on the customer's phone or watch |
| **Acquirers** | The acquirers you work with. Yuno integrates and certifies new acquirers on demand |
| **Instalments** | Where the acquirer offers them, such as parcelado in Brazil |

## Certification and compliance

You don't certify anything to use Tap to Pay. Yuno holds and maintains the certifications of the app that takes the payment; each acquirer certifies its connection once, with Yuno.

|  | What it is | Where it stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PCI MPoC** | The PCI standard for accepting cards and PINs on a standard phone | The Yuno Tap to Pay app on Android runs on an MPoC-certified solution. App updates and new features keep it; your brand is configuration, not a new certification |
| **Tap to Pay on iPhone** | Apple's own secure acceptance on iPhone | Apple provides the secure read and PIN entry; no MPoC applies on iPhone |
| **EMV contactless kernels** | The certified software that talks to the card, per brand | Part of the certified solution; Yuno never modifies it |
| **Level 3 / card-brand certification** | The full chain app to acquirer, tested end to end per acquirer and brand | Done once per acquirer, with Yuno. Adding an acquirer is a new certification; adding a merchant or a seller is not |
| **PCI DSS** | How card data is handled | Tap to Pay runs inside Yuno's PCI DSS Level 1 programme |
| **PCI PIN** | Transmitting, decrypting or translating PINs | The PIN is encrypted for the acquirer on the phone and travels to it; Yuno does not decrypt or translate it |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do sellers need a card reader or any extra hardware?">
    No. A supported Android phone or iPhone is enough. The customer taps directly on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the app carry our brand and not Yuno's?">
    Yes, that is the default. Name, logo, colours, screen texts and receipts are yours or your acquirer's, set from the dashboard. Changing them does not restart any certification.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do we need to change acquirers?">
    No. You keep your contracts and merchant IDs. Yuno routes between the acquirers you work with; if one is not integrated yet, Yuno integrates and certifies it on demand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer pay with a card that needs a PIN?">
    Yes. When the card, the amount or the market requires it, a secure PIN keypad appears on the screen. The PIN is encrypted as it is typed and never reaches your app or Yuno.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a declined tap be retried with another acquirer?">
    Not for the same sale. The card data and the PIN are protected for the acquirer chosen before the tap, and an authorisation only goes to one acquirer. Routing across sales is where the benefit is: cost, instalments and staying up when an acquirer is down.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Tap to Pay work offline?">
    No. Each sale needs a data connection on the phone. Wi-Fi or mobile data both work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can we use Tap to Pay and POS terminals together?">
    Yes. Both produce `IN_PERSON` payments in the same account, with the same routing rules, webhooks and reconciliation. Filter on `in_person.device_type` to tell them apart.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="We want the tap inside our own app, not in a second app. Is that possible?">
    The App to app type keeps your seller in your app until the tap, and returns the result to it. Running the tap fully inside your own app is possible for some cases; your Yuno contact will tell you what it involves for yours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can we launch in a market that is not listed?">
    Yes. Tell your Yuno contact the market and the acquirer. New markets are added under the same agreement; on iPhone, Apple decides in which countries Tap to Pay on iPhone is available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
