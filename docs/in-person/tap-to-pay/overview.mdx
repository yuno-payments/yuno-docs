Turn an Android phone or an iPhone into a payment terminal. Your customer taps a contactless card, phone or watch on the merchant's phone, the sale goes to the acquirer your rules choose, and it lands in the same Yuno account as your online and POS terminal payments.

<Frame caption="The phone is the terminal: the customer taps, Yuno routes the sale.">
  <img src="/images/in-person/tap-to-pay/hero-tap.png" alt="A customer taps a contactless card on a phone showing a 340.00 charge, routing to the best acquirer" />
</Frame>

<Info>
  **Two words used on these pages.** The **phone** is the merchant's Android phone or iPhone that takes the payment; the customer taps on it. The **Yuno Tap to Pay app** is the app that runs the payment on the phone. Sellers download it from the App Store or Google Play and, once they sign in, it shows your brand or your acquirer's.
</Info>

Every tap is a Yuno `payment` with `channel: IN_PERSON`, the same identifier, statuses, events and webhooks as an online payment or a sale on a POS terminal. What changes is the entry mode (`CONTACTLESS`) and the device data that comes with it.

## How it works

- **The sale runs in the Yuno Tap to Pay app** by default, either on its own or opened from your app with the amount and your reference, which Yuno has already certified, so you don't certify anything. Merchants who want the tap inside their own app can use the Yuno Tap to Pay SDK and certify their own app. See [Integration types](/docs/in-person/tap-to-pay/ways-to-integrate).
- **Yuno picks the acquirer** with your routing rules before the customer taps, so the card data and the PIN are protected for that acquirer from the start.
- **The customer taps** their card, phone or watch on the merchant's phone. The card is read by a certified contactless kernel inside a protected environment on the phone; the card number, the cryptogram and the PIN never reach the merchant's app or your servers.
- **The acquirer authorises**, and the result appears on the phone in a couple of seconds. Yuno records the payment, sends your webhooks and, later, reconciles it against the acquirer's settlement.

## How a sale flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="The seller enters the amount" icon="keyboard">
    In the Yuno Tap to Pay app, or your app passes the amount and your order reference to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yuno chooses the acquirer" icon="route">
    From your rules: merchant, market, card brand, amount and instalments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer taps" icon="hand-pointer">
    Card, phone or watch on the phone's tap area. The PIN on the screen when it is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The sale is approved or declined" icon="circle-check">
    On the phone, and as a Yuno payment with `channel: IN_PERSON` and the same webhooks as online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yuno reconciles" icon="scale-balanced">
    The acquirer's settlement file is matched per merchant and acquirer, and the differences are listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full sequence, including where to tap, PIN, refunds and what happens when a sale fails, is on [Payment flow](/docs/in-person/tap-to-pay/payment-flow).

## Coverage at a glance

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Platforms** | Android phones and tablets with NFC; iPhone XS or later |
| **Markets** | Mexico, Brazil and Ecuador; more markets on demand. On iPhone, only where Apple offers Tap to Pay on iPhone ([Apple's list of supported regions](https://developer.apple.com/tap-to-pay/regions/)) |
| **Card brands** | Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and local brands; Elo coming soon |
| **Acquirers** | The acquirers you work with; Yuno integrates and certifies new acquirers on demand |
| **Payment methods** | Contactless cards and mobile wallets |

Details and compliance: [Coverage and FAQ](/docs/in-person/tap-to-pay/coverage-and-faq).
