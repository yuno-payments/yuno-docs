Turn an Android phone or an iPhone into a payment terminal. Your customer taps a contactless card, phone or watch on the merchant's phone, the sale goes to the acquirer your rules choose, and it lands in the same Yuno account as your online and POS terminal payments.

<Info>
  **Two words used on these pages.** The **phone** is the merchant's Android phone or iPhone that takes the payment; the customer taps on it. The **Yuno Tap to Pay app** is the app that runs the payment on the phone, under your brand or your acquirer's.
</Info>

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="No hardware">
    The merchant's own phone is the terminal. Nothing to buy, ship or maintain, and a new seller can take payments the day they sign up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Android and iPhone">
    One product on both platforms, with the same payment, events and reconciliation whichever phone took the sale.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your brand, your acquirers">
    White label by default, and multi-acquirer routing with the acquirers you already work with.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every tap is a Yuno `payment` with `channel: IN_PERSON`, the same identifier, statuses, events and webhooks as an online payment or a sale on a POS terminal. What changes is the entry mode (`CONTACTLESS`) and the device data that comes with it.

## How it works

- **The sale runs in the Yuno Tap to Pay app** by default, either on its own or opened from your app with the amount and your reference, so your app needs no security review. Merchants who want the tap inside their own app can use the Yuno Tap to Pay SDK and take on the review. See [Integration types](/docs/in-person/tap-to-pay/ways-to-integrate).
- **Yuno picks the acquirer** with your routing rules before the customer taps, so the card data and the PIN are protected for that acquirer from the start.
- **The customer taps** their card, phone or watch on the merchant's phone. The card is read by a certified contactless kernel inside a protected environment on the phone; the card number, the cryptogram and the PIN never reach the merchant's app or your servers.
- **The acquirer authorises**, and the result appears on the phone in a couple of seconds. Yuno records the payment, sends your webhooks and, later, reconciles it against the acquirer's settlement.

## How a sale flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="The seller enters the amount">
    In the Yuno Tap to Pay app, or your app passes the amount and your order reference to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yuno chooses the acquirer">
    From your rules: merchant, market, card brand, amount and instalments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer taps">
    Card, phone or watch on the phone's tap area. The PIN on the screen when it is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The sale is approved or declined">
    On the phone, and as a Yuno payment with `channel: IN_PERSON` and the same webhooks as online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yuno reconciles">
    The acquirer's settlement file is matched per merchant and acquirer, and the differences are listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full sequence, including where to tap, PIN, voids, refunds and what happens when a sale fails, is on [Payment flow](/docs/in-person/tap-to-pay/payment-flow).
