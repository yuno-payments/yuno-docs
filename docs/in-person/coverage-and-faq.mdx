## Coverage

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Country** | Mexico first. Other markets follow per acquirer certification. |
| **Acquirers** | BBVA, then EVO / Global Payments. Additional acquirers on request. |
| **Terminals** | Ingenico AXIUM 5000 and 7000 first; Verifone next. New vendors are added as adapters to the same app. |
| **Methods** | Chip and PIN, contactless. QR (CoDi) on the terminal in a later phase. |
| **Timeline** | Build and certification Q4 2026. First merchant live in Q1 2027. Wider availability from Q2 2027. |
| **Compliance** | EMV certification per acquirer and card brand; PCI DSS scope extended to card present. The PIN is never handled by Yuno. |

Not in the first release: offline acceptance while the acquirer is unreachable, tips and cashback on the terminal, tap to pay on phones, subscriptions started in store, and alternative payment methods on the terminal. Each is planned; ask your Yuno contact for dates.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do we need to buy new terminals?">
    No. Yuno's app runs on the terminals you own. If you are mid-migration to a new fleet, that is the easiest moment to add it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do we need to change acquirers?">
    No. You keep your contracts and your rates. Yuno connects to each acquirer you use and routes between them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Yuno touch the card data or the PIN?">
    The card is read and the PIN is entered on the terminal's certified hardware, encrypted to the acquirer. Yuno receives the result and the identifiers needed to record and reconcile the sale, not the PIN.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the store loses its connection to Yuno?">
    The terminal keeps the latest routing rules and continues to authorise directly with the acquirer. Sales are reported to Yuno when the connection returns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a declined payment be retried with another acquirer?">
    Not in store. The PIN is encrypted for the acquirer chosen before the customer typed it, so the result of that sale is final. Routing across sales is where the benefit is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same account as our online payments?">
    Yes. Same account, same payment resource, same webhooks, same dashboard. In-store sales carry `channel: IN_PERSON` and terminal data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We are a platform and our merchants own the terminals. How do we integrate?">
    Two options: reconciliation first, connecting your merchants' acquirers today, and the Cloud integration type, where your server creates the sale and a registered terminal in the store collects it. The second is being designed with pilot partners now.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
