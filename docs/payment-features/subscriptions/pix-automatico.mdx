Yuno bills a Brazilian Pix Automático mandate as an ordinary subscription: you enroll the mandate
first, then create the subscription against the resulting `vaulted_token` — or, with a plan, set up
the mandate and the subscription in the first payment
([Signing up from a plan](#signing-up-from-a-plan-with-the-first-payment)). With EBANX you can also
collect it through the `PIX` payment method and charge it yourself
([Pix with vault on success](#pix-with-vault-on-success)). Three things behave
differently from a card subscription, and all three are set by the Pix rail rather than by your
request — the billing cadences the mandate can express, the date the debit instruction is handed to
the rail, and the retry ladder that runs when a debit is refused.

## Creating a Pix Automático subscription

Send `payment_method.type` as `PIX_AUTOMATIC` with the `vaulted_token` of an already-enrolled
mandate, exactly as you would for a card:

```json Create a monthly Pix Automático subscription
{
  "name": "Streaming BR",
  "amount": {
    "currency": "BRL",
    "value": 29.90
  },
  "frequency": {
    "type": "MONTH",
    "value": 1
  },
  "customer_payer": {
    "id": "a15783f1-94e1-4ec0-ab8b-306f8517bcff"
  },
  "payment_method": {
    "type": "PIX_AUTOMATIC",
    "vaulted_token": "296dff7f-a6cb-4ce2-bf42-c51f1b356a2d"
  }
}
```

`payment_method.type` is matched case-insensitively, so `pix_automatic` resolves to the same payment
method — and to the same cadence rules — as `PIX_AUTOMATIC`.

Except for [Pix with vault on success](#pix-with-vault-on-success), everything on this page
describes subscriptions **Yuno executes**: `frequency.execution` is `YUNO`,
so Yuno generates each debit instruction on schedule. On the one-call route with inline terms,
`frequency.execution` is sent explicitly and has no default; with a `plan_id` it defaults to `YUNO` — see
[Start a subscription with the first payment](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/start-with-payment#execution-decides-who-bills).
Where `frequency.execution` is `MERCHANT` you generate each charge yourself, the scheduling and
retry behavior below does not apply, and the subscription cannot
[schedule a cancellation](/reference/subscriptions/cancel-subscription#scheduling-a-cancellation-instead).

## Signing up from a plan with the first payment

The call above needs a mandate that is already enrolled. To set up the mandate and create the
subscription in one call, send [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment) with
`payment_method.type` `PIX_AUTOMATIC` and `subscription.plan_id`. The request is described in
[Create a subscription from a plan](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/start-with-payment#create-a-subscription-from-a-plan). The
customer approves the Pix Automático request in their bank app, and that approval authorizes the
mandate.

What the first payment does depends on the plan:

* **Plan without a trial:** the payment is the first charge, at the plan's price. When the customer
  approves, the payment succeeds and counts as billing cycle 1. The next debit is one cadence later.
  The subscription stays `CREATED` until the provider confirms the mandate, then becomes active.
  If the first payment is refused, the subscription stays `CREATED` and is never billed; cancel it
  with [Cancel Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/cancel-subscription). If it expires, a late
  approval from the bank can still complete it and activate the subscription; cancel it only if you
  don't want that.
* **Plan with a free trial:** send the regular price as the payment `amount`. The mandate setup is
  sent to the provider at `0.00`, and the subscription's amount stays the regular price. The
  subscription starts `TRIALING` at billing cycle 0 and bills nothing until the customer approves
  the mandate. The trial starts when the customer approves (or at `availability.start_at`, if that is
  later), and the first paid debit comes after the trial.

The plan must fit what one Pix Automático mandate can express: either no trial, or a **free** trial
on the same cadence as the regular phase. Anything else is rejected before the provider is called:

```json Priced trial, or a trial on another cadence
{
  "code": "INVALID_PARAMETERS",
  "messages": [
    "Pix plans support only free trials with the regular billing cadence."
  ]
}
```

### While a free-trial setup awaits approval

These rules apply to free-trial plans only, where nothing is collected until the customer approves.

* **The provider refuses the setup:** Yuno cancels the subscription. It is never billed.
* **The customer never approves:** Yuno cancels the subscription 31 days after the payment was
  created. If an approval still arrives after that, Yuno asks the provider to revoke the mandate
  and logs an alert.
* **The payment shows `EXPIRED`:** this alone does not cancel the subscription. The bank can still
  deliver an approval after the QR code expires, so Yuno keeps waiting until the 31-day limit.
* Pause, resume, reactivate and [Change Subscription Plan](/reference/subscriptions/change-subscription-plan)
  are rejected with `400 INVALID_STATE` until the mandate is approved. If the setup is canceled
  without an approval, they stay rejected.

## Pix with vault on success

With EBANX you can also collect Pix Automático through the `PIX` payment method: the first payment
creates the recurring authorization and saves it as a `vaulted_token`, and later charges use that
token. `PIX_AUTOMATIC` remains a separate payment method; enable in your checkout only the ones you
want to offer. Here your system decides when to charge (`frequency.execution` `MERCHANT`).

**1. First payment.** Send `payment_method.vault_on_success: true` and a `subscription` block that
describes what the customer authorizes:

* `frequency.type` and `frequency.value`: the same cadences as
  [Pix Automático](#accepted-billing-cadences) (`WEEK`, `MONTH`, `QUARTER`, `HALF_YEAR` or `YEAR`
  with value `1`), plus `MONTH` `3`, `6` or `12` for quarterly, half-yearly or yearly.
* `frequency.execution` `MERCHANT`: your system decides when to charge. Yuno does not reject
  `YUNO`, but this flow is documented for `MERCHANT` only.
* `availability` and `amount`, with `amount.type`. A request without one of them is rejected with
  `HTTP 400` before any charge. With `amount.type` `FIXED`, `subscription.amount.value` must equal
  the payment `amount.value`; use `VARIABLE` to authorize later charges larger than the first one.

Yuno sends EBANX the larger of the payment `amount` and `subscription.amount` (when both use the
same currency) as the authorization's minimum limit. When approving, the customer sets the maximum per-charge amount in their bank app,
and it cannot be lower than that value.

The customer referenced by `customer_payer.id` must have a first and last name, an email and a CPF
or CNPJ in `document`. Yuno sends them to EBANX to create the authorization and on every later
charge; it does not validate them first, and EBANX declines the payment if they are missing.

```json Create the first Pix payment
{
  "account_id": "493e9374-510a-4201-9e09-de669d75f256",
  "merchant_order_id": "order-0001",
  "description": "Monthly plan",
  "country": "BR",
  "amount": {
    "currency": "BRL",
    "value": 15.00
  },
  "workflow": "DIRECT",
  "customer_payer": {
    "id": "a3b3a0f4-1f1e-4a19-9a2a-6f0f2d6bbd11"
  },
  "payment_method": {
    "type": "PIX",
    "vault_on_success": true
  },
  "subscription": {
    "frequency": {
      "type": "MONTH",
      "value": 1,
      "execution": "MERCHANT"
    },
    "availability": {
      "finish_at": "2027-10-27T00:00:00Z"
    },
    "amount": {
      "currency": "BRL",
      "value": 15.00,
      "type": "FIXED"
    }
  }
}
```

Without the `subscription` block, or with a frequency other than the ones above (for example
`MONTH` `2`), the payment is created as `DECLINED` and the provider is not called. The
transaction's `response_message` starts with
`vault_on_success on PIX requires a subscription to enroll the recurring authorization` and lists
the accepted frequencies.

The response is `READY_TO_PAY` (`sub_status` `CREATED`) with a redirect: send the customer to
`payment_method.payment_method_detail.bank_transfer.redirect_url`, the EBANX page that shows the
Pix QR code. The response has no QR code field for you to render. The customer pays once in their
bank app, which settles this payment and approves the recurring authorization.

The `subscription` block also creates a Yuno subscription, returned as `subscription_code`. Charge
the authorization with the vaulted token as shown below, not with `subscription.id`.

**2. The vaulted token.** Yuno saves the customer's Pix payment method when this payment is
created, before the customer approves it. Its token is in
[Retrieve Enrolled Payment Methods](/reference/payment-methods-direct-workflow/retrieve-enrolled-payment-methods-api)
right away, and in `transactions[].payment_method.vaulted_token` from
[Retrieve Payment](/reference/payments/retrieve-payment-by-id-v2) once the payment reaches
`SUCCEEDED`. Charge it only after this payment is `SUCCEEDED`: until the customer approves, a charge
with the token is declined. If the customer had already paid with Pix without `vault_on_success`,
Retrieve Enrolled Payment Methods does not return the saved Pix: read the token from Retrieve
Payment.

Each customer has one saved Pix payment method, and its `vaulted_token` does not change. A new
first payment with `vault_on_success` does not replace the saved authorization while that payment
is pending or after it is declined: charges with the token keep debiting the authorization the
customer already approved. When the new payment reaches `SUCCEEDED`, the saved Pix switches to its
authorization, and later charges debit that one.

In the EBANX sandbox, an authorization that is not approved within about three minutes of the
payment's creation stops being pending: EBANX reports it as `cancelled` or `revoked`. A payment
approved after that still reaches `SUCCEEDED`, but its authorization is not accepted, so charges
with the token are declined with `BP-DR-182` in the transaction's `response_message` until the
customer approves a new first payment. When you test, approve the first payment within three
minutes.

**3. Later charges.** Charge the stored authorization with the vaulted token. No QR code and no
customer approval are needed:

```json Charge with the vaulted token
{
  "account_id": "493e9374-510a-4201-9e09-de669d75f256",
  "merchant_order_id": "order-0002",
  "description": "Monthly plan — November",
  "country": "BR",
  "amount": {
    "currency": "BRL",
    "value": 15.00
  },
  "workflow": "DIRECT",
  "customer_payer": {
    "id": "a3b3a0f4-1f1e-4a19-9a2a-6f0f2d6bbd11"
  },
  "payment_method": {
    "type": "PIX",
    "vaulted_token": "665dfa97-3a64-4173-8552-58fee66abf80"
  }
}
```

A Pix Automático charge is a scheduled debit: the create response returns `PENDING` with
`sub_status` `PENDING_PROVIDER_CONFIRMATION`, and Yuno asks EBANX to debit the customer on a due
date two days after the request. The final status arrives by webhook: `SUCCEEDED` when the debit
settles, otherwise `DECLINED` or `EXPIRED`. EBANX does not retry a failed debit on authorizations
created through Yuno. If EBANX rejects the charge when it is created, for example because the
customer has not approved the authorization, the payment is `DECLINED` in the create response and
no money moves.

**In the checkout.** When you create the checkout session with the customer's `customer_id` and Pix
is enabled in your checkout, the saved Pix appears as its own option, labeled with the last four
digits of the customer's document (for example `Pix ****6893`), next to the regular Pix option that
shows a new QR code. Paying with the saved option needs no QR code: the SDK shows the payment as in
progress, and the final status arrives by webhook.

**Authorizations created before Yuno.** If you already hold Pix Automático authorizations created
with EBANX, contact your Yuno representative before charging them through Yuno.

## Accepted billing cadences

These rules apply to `PIX_AUTOMATIC`. For `PIX` with vault on success, see
[Pix with vault on success](#pix-with-vault-on-success).

A Pix Automático mandate can only express the periodicities the Pix rail defines, so a cadence the
mandate could not execute is rejected when the subscription is created rather than failing at the
first debit.

Accepted today: **`WEEK × 1`, `MONTH × 1`, `QUARTER × 1`, `HALF_YEAR × 1` and `YEAR × 1`** —
`frequency.type` is one of those five and `frequency.value` is exactly `1`. Daily does not exist for
Pix Automático — this is a BCB restriction on the rail itself, not a Yuno or Adyen limitation.

| `frequency` | Result |
|---|---|
| `WEEK`, value `1` | Accepted |
| `MONTH`, value `1` | Accepted |
| `QUARTER`, value `1` | Accepted |
| `HALF_YEAR`, value `1` | Accepted |
| `YEAR`, value `1` | Accepted, through a plan only — see [Yearly needs a plan](#yearly-needs-a-plan) |
| `WEEK`, `MONTH`, `QUARTER`, `HALF_YEAR` or `YEAR` with any other value | `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS` |
| `DAY`, any value | `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS` |

The rejection is the same on every path, `HTTP 400`:

```json Rejected cadence
{
  "code": "INVALID_PARAMETERS",
  "messages": [
    "Pix Automático only supports weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly frequencies with value 1."
  ]
}
```

A subscription billed on a fixed date — the `billing_date` object instead of `frequency` — is not
affected: it has no `frequency.type` for the rule to judge, so `PREPAID`, `POSTDATE`, `DAY` and
`PLAN_CREATION_DATE` all stay available on Pix Automático.

### Where the rule applies

The cadence is judged whenever a subscription resolves to a Pix Automático payment method — on the
requested cadence for an inline `frequency`, and on the plan's cadence when the subscription is
created from a `plan_id`. It is judged again on the target cadence of a plan change: on
[Change Subscription Plan](/reference/subscriptions/change-subscription-plan), and on
`PATCH /v1/subscriptions/{id}` with a `plan_change` block, for both `NEXT_BILLING_CYCLE` and
`IMMEDIATE`.

Plans themselves are unaffected. A plan on any cadence stays creatable — the rule gates the
subscription, not the plan — so a `DAY` plan is only refused at the moment a Pix Automático
subscription tries to bill from it.

### Existing subscriptions are never re-judged

Only the cadence being requested is judged; a subscription already in flight is never re-judged.
A Pix Automático subscription created before this rule existed keeps billing on its original
cadence, and can still be moved onto a plan — that move is judged on the target plan's cadence, so
it succeeds whenever the target is one of the five accepted cadences.

### Yearly needs a plan

Sending `frequency.type` as `YEAR` inline — with no `plan_id` and no phases — is refused by a
separate, older rule that applies to every payment method, not just Pix Automático:

```json Inline YEAR, any payment method
{
  "code": "BAD_REQUEST",
  "messages": [
    "YEAR is invalid for frequency.type field"
  ]
}
```

To bill a Pix Automático subscription yearly, create it from a plan whose frequency is `YEAR × 1`.
The Pix cadence rule accepts it; only the inline route is closed. This does not apply to
[Pix with vault on success](#pix-with-vault-on-success), which accepts `YEAR` `1` in the payment's
`subscription` block.

## The scheduling date

A Pix debit is not taken on demand — the instruction has to reach the rail ahead of the debit date.
For a Pix Automático subscription that Yuno executes, Yuno schedules the instruction **four days
before** the debit date and reports that moment as `billing_cycles.scheduling_date`.

`scheduling_date` is exactly `billing_cycles.next_at` minus four days:

```json billing_cycles on a Pix Automático subscription
{
  "billing_cycles": {
    "total": null,
    "current": 0,
    "paid": 0,
    "next_at": "2026-12-01T00:00:00Z",
    "scheduling_date": "2026-11-27T00:00:00Z"
  }
}
```

On every other payment method the key is **absent, not `null`** — a card subscription's
`billing_cycles` carries `total`, `current`, `paid` and `next_at` and nothing else. Read it from
[Retrieve Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/retrieve-subscription), from the items of
[List Subscriptions](/reference/subscriptions/list-subscriptions), and from subscription webhook
payloads, which carry it inside the same `billing_cycles` block.

`subscription.close_to_renewal` moves with it: for these subscriptions the notice is counted from
`scheduling_date` rather than from `next_at`, so it arrives four days earlier than it would for a
card subscription with the same `next_at` and the same `renewal_notification_days`.

When the scheduling date has already passed at the moment the subscription is created — the first
debit is less than four days out — there is nothing to wait for and the instruction is scheduled
straight away instead.

<Note>
**Allowlisted organizations only**

The four-day scheduling lead and the `scheduling_date` field are enabled per organization. Where
they are not enabled, `scheduling_date` is absent from every read and the instruction is scheduled
on Yuno's standard renewal lead instead. Contact your Yuno representative to have it enabled.
</Note>

## The retry ladder

A refused Pix debit is retried on the Pix rail's own terms rather than on the
[Smart Retries](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/retries) schedule. Yuno reports the ladder it
applies on the subscription's `retries` object:

| Field | Value on a Pix Automático subscription |
|---|---|
| `profile` | `PIX_AUTOMATIC` |
| `max_attempts` | `3` |
| `min_gap_hours` | `24` |
| `until` | `billing_cycles.next_at` plus 7 days |

`amount` reads `3` on these subscriptions rather than the card default of `6`, matching
`max_attempts`. The four fields above are **absent, not `null`**, on `CARD` and on every other
payment method.

### `min_gap_hours` counts debit dates, not dispatches

`min_gap_hours: 24` is the gap between **debit dates** — the ladder runs on **D, D+1, D+2, D+3**,
the original debit on D and up to three retries on the three days after it. It is not a gap between
dispatch attempts, and it does not mean Yuno waits 24 hours before sending the next instruction.

When the provider refuses an instruction synchronously, the instruction for the next debit date can
go out seconds later, so against a provider that rejects everything the whole ladder can be
dispatched almost at once. That is the ladder working as specified, not a fault. Check the **debit
dates** carried on the attempts — D, D+1, D+2, D+3 — not the times at which they were sent.

### The ladder is not configurable

`retries.retry_on_decline` is the one input these subscriptions accept, in both directions: `false`
suppresses the ladder entirely and leaves a single attempt per cycle. Sending `amount`, `strategy`,
`schedule`, `stop_on_hard_decline` or `cancel_on_exhausted_retries` on a Pix Automático subscription
— at creation or on [Update Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/update-subscription) — is
rejected:

```json Configuring the Pix ladder
{
  "code": "INVALID_PARAMETERS",
  "messages": [
    "Retries for PIX_AUTOMATIC follow the Pix rail (max 3, 24 h apart, within 7 days of the debit date) and cannot be configured."
  ]
}
```

## Fields Yuno computes

`billing_cycles.scheduling_date` and the `retries.stopped`, `retries.attempts_made`,
`retries.outcome` and `retries.next_retry_at` snapshot are computed by Yuno from the subscription's
own state and are ignored on create and update — sending them changes nothing, and the values you
read back are always Yuno's. See
[The Subscription Object](/reference/subscriptions/the-subscription-object) for the full field
reference.
