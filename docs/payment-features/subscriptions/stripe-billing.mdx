Keep Stripe Billing as your subscription engine (plans, invoices, billing cycles, dunning) while Yuno processes every charge through your own providers and routing rules. Your subscription logic doesn't change: only the checkout moves to Yuno, and Yuno takes care of the rest.

## Two paths

There are two ways to move subscriptions onto this bridge, depending on whether the subscriber is new or already active on Stripe:

| Path | Who | What happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| New bridged signup | Customers creating a subscription or converting onto the bridge | Create Stripe Customer + Subscription with `payment_behavior: default_incomplete`, then collect the first payment through the **Yuno SDK**. Yuno vaults the card, attaches it to the subscription as its default payment method and reports the payment to Stripe. Stripe marks the first invoice as paid and activates the subscription. |
| Existing Stripe subscribers | Subscribers already charging on Stripe (`sub_` active) | Yuno copies the card into its vault via **Stripe Forwarding** (no charge). When you ask Yuno to activate a cohort, renewals start going through Yuno. Stripe keeps billing until you flip each subscription. |

New signups are documented in the [step-by-step](#step-1-create-the-subscription-in-stripe) below. For existing subscribers, see [Existing subscribers](#existing-subscribers-migrate-the-base).

<Info>
  **Hybrid is per subscription.** Stripe-processed and Yuno-bridged subscriptions can coexist on the same Stripe account. Enabling the bridge does not force every subscriber onto the Yuno SDK overnight.
</Info>

## How it works

Stripe's [third-party payment processing](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/third-party-payment-processing) mode lets an external processor execute the charges for your Billing subscriptions. With Yuno:

1. **Signup:** your customer pays the first charge through your Yuno checkout (3DS, anti-fraud and smart routing included). Yuno securely vaults the card.
2. **Activation:** Yuno automatically registers the payment in Stripe, links the vaulted card to the subscription, and Stripe activates it. Stripe never sees the card.
3. **Renewals:** when Stripe issues a renewal invoice, Yuno charges the vaulted card as a merchant-initiated transaction (with network-compliant stored credentials) and reports the outcome back to Stripe. The invoice is marked paid automatically.
4. **Recovery:** failed renewals follow the retry schedule you configure in Stripe; each retry is executed by Yuno across your provider cascade.
5. **Refunds:** refund through Yuno as usual: Yuno reports it to Stripe and creates the credit note.

Your backend keeps listening to the same Stripe webhooks you use today (`invoice.paid`, `customer.subscription.updated`, …) for entitlements. No changes there.

## Prerequisites

* A Yuno account with your providers connected and routing published.
* A Stripe account with Billing and **third-party payment processing enabled** (request it from Stripe).
* Onboarding with Yuno: share a **restricted API key** from your Stripe account with your Yuno contact so Yuno can register the webhook endpoint and report payments. Yuno stores it encrypted and uses it only for your account.

## Step 1: Create the subscription in Stripe

Create the Customer and Subscription in Stripe exactly as you do today (use `payment_behavior: default_incomplete`). Keep the `customer` and `subscription` ids: you'll send them to Yuno in the next step.

<Warning>
  Create the subscription at checkout time and collect the payment in the same session: Stripe requires the first payment to be reported within **23 hours** of the subscription's creation.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Collect the first payment with Yuno

Run your normal Yuno checkout and create the payment from your backend with two additions: `vault_on_success` and the `external_subscription` object linking the payment to your Stripe subscription (see [Create payment](/reference/payments/create-payment)).

**The `token` in the request must come from the Yuno SDK.** This is the one-time token returned by your checkout (Seamless, Lite, or Secure Fields). Stripe never sees the card; the card is vaulted in Yuno and only a reference is stored in your Stripe account.

```json
{
  "account_id": "<your_account_id>",
  "merchant_order_id": "<your_order_id>",
  "description": "Pro plan, first month",
  "country": "US",
  "amount": { "currency": "USD", "value": 99.00 },
  "customer_payer": { "id": "<yuno_customer_id>" },
  "payment_method": {
    "type": "CARD",
    "token": "<one_time_token_from_sdk>",
    "vault_on_success": true
  },
  "external_subscription": {
    "id": "sub_1QwZzz",
    "customer_id": "cus_RplXYZ"
  }
}
```

That's it. When the payment is approved, Yuno automatically: creates a payment method reference in your Stripe account (no card data), attaches it to the subscription as its default payment method, and reports the payment so Stripe marks the first invoice as paid and activates the subscription.

## Free trials

When signup starts a **trial**, there is no first charge. The trigger is the **$0 card verification** of the enrollment flow instead. Create the Stripe subscription with `trial_end` as usual, then enroll the card through the [Enroll payment method](/reference/enroll-payment-method-api) endpoint with the `external_subscription` object nested inside `verify`:

<Info>
  This enrollment flow is for **new trialing subscriptions**. If you need to migrate an existing, actively paying subscription to Yuno, see [Existing subscribers](#existing-subscribers-migrate-the-base). Do not ask existing paid subscribers to re-enter their card for a verification; use Stripe Forwarding instead.
</Info>

```json
{
  "account_id": "<your_account_id>",
  "country": "US",
  "type": "CARD",
  "workflow": "DIRECT",
  "card_data": { "...": "..." },
  "verify": {
    "vault_on_success": true,
    "currency": "USD",
    "external_subscription": {
      "id": "sub_1QwZzz",
      "customer_id": "cus_RplXYZ"
    }
  }
}
```

<Info>
  Use the same currency for the verification as the subscription's: renewals are charged in the subscription's currency.
</Info>

On a successful verification Yuno links the vaulted card to the trialing subscription (its `default_payment_method` in Stripe becomes the Yuno reference) and nothing is charged or reported: no invoice is due. At trial end, Stripe issues the first real invoice and Yuno charges it with network-compliant stored credentials, exactly like any renewal.

## Renewals: nothing to do

Every renewal is automatic: Stripe finalizes the invoice → Yuno charges the vaulted card → Stripe marks it paid. You'll see each charge as a normal payment in your Yuno dashboard and reports, and the invoice lifecycle in Stripe as always.

## Failed payments and recovery

Configure a **custom retry schedule** in Stripe (Smart Retries are not available in third-party processing mode: a Stripe limitation). Each scheduled retry triggers a new charge attempt through Yuno, executed across your routing and provider cascade. If all retries are exhausted, your Stripe dunning configuration decides the subscription's fate, as usual.

## Refunds

Refund through the Yuno API or dashboard as with any payment. Yuno reports the refund to Stripe and creates the corresponding **credit note** so your Stripe books stay consistent. Partial refunds are supported.

## Changing the customer's card

Collect a new payment (or run a new verification, for trialing subscriptions) sending the same `external_subscription.id`. Yuno vaults the new card and switches the subscription's default payment method in Stripe automatically. Renewals continue without interruption.

## Existing subscribers (migrate the base)

If you already have active Stripe subscriptions, you do not need to ask every subscriber to re-enter their card in the Yuno SDK. Yuno can copy the payment credentials from Stripe into its vault using **Stripe Forwarding**, then flip each subscription to charge through Yuno when you are ready.

<Warning>
  **Contact your Yuno team to schedule base migration.** This path is not self-serve from the Dashboard today. Yuno runs the copy job and activation on your behalf after onboarding.
</Warning>

### Prerequisites for base migration

Before Yuno can migrate existing subscribers:

- Complete the standard [bridge onboarding](#prerequisites) (Stripe third-party mode enabled, restricted key shared with Yuno).
- Enable **Stripe Forwarding** toward Yuno on your Stripe account. This lets Stripe securely send card data to Yuno's vault without exposing it to your backend.
- The restricted API key must include read access to subscriptions, customers, and charges, plus write access for Forwarding.

### How the copy job works

For each active subscription you want to migrate:

1. Yuno creates a matching customer in its vault.
2. Stripe Forwarding sends the card data to Yuno, where it is vaulted and enrolled.
3. Yuno harvests the network transaction id from the most recent successful charge (this is required for compliant merchant-initiated transactions on renewals).
4. Yuno maps the Stripe subscription id (`sub_...`) to the vaulted token.

**This copy does not charge the card.** It does not change who bills the subscription. Stripe continues to process renewals until you activate the cohort.

### Cohort activation

When you are ready to move a group of subscriptions to Yuno:

1. Yuno attaches its custom payment method reference as the `default_payment_method` on each subscription in Stripe.
2. The next renewal Stripe issues goes through Yuno (merchant-initiated transaction with stored credentials), and Yuno reports the result back to Stripe.

**Rollback:** if you need to revert a cohort, point `default_payment_method` back to the original Stripe card. The next renewal goes through Stripe again.

### What does not migrate cleanly

- **Link-saved credentials:** cards saved through Stripe Link stay Stripe-native. Forwarding does not cover Link tokens.
- **Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay):** wallet credentials often need a separate migration decision. Discuss with your Yuno team whether to re-collect wallet credentials through the SDK.
- **Card fingerprints:** the Yuno fingerprint differs from the Stripe fingerprint for the same card. If your systems rely on fingerprint matching, plan a mapping.

### When to use SDK re-collect instead

If Forwarding cannot cover a cohort (for example, a subscriber's original card expired, or you need to migrate a Link-saved credential), fall back to the same flow as [Changing the customer's card](#changing-the-customer’s-card): the subscriber enters their new card in the Yuno SDK, and Yuno updates the subscription's default payment method automatically.

## Not supported in this mode

Per Stripe's third-party processing mode: Stripe Checkout, the Stripe-hosted invoice page, Smart Retries and recovery emails, Stripe-managed disputes, and Stripe-initiated refunds. Embedded Stripe pricing tables are not compatible (the checkout is Yuno's).

**Hybrid coexistence is fully supported.** Subscriptions that stay processed by Stripe and subscriptions that go through the Yuno bridge can coexist on the same Stripe account. You do not have to migrate every subscriber at once. Each subscription is independent: some can remain on Stripe's native processing while others use the third-party bridge.
