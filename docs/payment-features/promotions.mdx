A promotion is a discount you define once, for example "10% off with BBVA credit cards in October". Yuno checks each payment against your promotions, shows the discounted price at checkout, and charges exactly that price.

<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

Promotions is off by default. Yuno turns it on for your **organization**, and it then works on the accounts of that organization. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager to enable it, in sandbox first and then in production.

Until it's enabled, no promotion applies to your payments: [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote) answers `204`, and the checkout shows the regular price.
</Warning>

## How it works

A promotion reaches a payment in three steps. The **quote** is the link between them: it's the price the customer saw, and the payment charges that price.

1. **Create the promotion.** Use the Yuno Dashboard or [Create Promotion](/reference/promotions/create-promotion). You set the discount, the dates, and the conditions a payment must meet (card BIN or BIN range, card type, payment method, country, currency, minimum or maximum amount).
2. **Get a quote.** Before the payment, Yuno prices it: which promotion applies, the original amount, the discount, and the charge. The answer carries `quote_id` and `promotions` (one entry today, with `id`, `name`, `description`, and `discount_amount`). With the Yuno checkout this happens by itself. With a direct API integration, you call [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote).
3. **Create the payment with the quote.** Send the original `amount` and `additional_data.order.discounts: [{ "quote_id": "qte_…" }]` on [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment). Yuno charges the quoted total and returns the breakdown.

<Note>
**A promotion applies only when the payment carries a quote id on a discount line**

Yuno never discounts a payment by itself. A payment without a `quote_id` on `additional_data.order.discounts` is charged in full, exactly as today, even when a promotion would have matched it.
</Note>

## Enable promotions for your organization

1. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager to enable Promotions for your organization. Start with sandbox.
2. Once it's on, create your promotions from the Dashboard or the API. Each promotion belongs to one `account_id`. If you run the same campaign on several accounts, create it once per account.
3. Test a payment in sandbox with a card that meets the conditions, then ask for production.

Yuno can also turn Promotions off for an organization. When it's off, quotes answer `204` and the checkout shows the regular price, so the customer never sees a discount that the payment doesn't honour.

## Create a promotion

```json Create Promotion
curl --request POST \
     --url https://api-sandbox.y.uno/v1/promotions \
     --header 'X-Idempotency-Key: <Your idempotency-key>' \
     --header 'content-type: application/json' \
     --header 'private-secret-key: <Your private-secret-key>' \
     --header 'public-api-key: <Your public-api-key>' \
     --data '
{
    "account_id": "493e9374-510a-4201-9e09-de669d75f256",
    "name": "BBVA 10% off - Mother'\''s Day",
    "schedule": { "starts_at": "2026-10-01T03:00:00Z", "ends_at": "2026-11-01T02:59:59Z" },
    "redemption": { "mode": "AUTOMATIC" },
    "discount": { "type": "PERCENTAGE", "value": 10 },
    "conditions": {
        "bins": { "values": ["457896", "40263412"], "ranges": [{ "start": "45780000", "end": "45789999" }] },
        "payment_methods": { "values": ["CARD"] },
        "card_types": { "values": ["CREDIT"] },
        "countries": { "values": ["AR"] },
        "amounts": { "values": [{ "currency": "ARS", "min": 10000.00, "max": null }] }
    }
}'
```

Things to know before you create one:

* **Promotions can't be edited.** The only change is to [disable](/reference/promotions/disable-promotion) one, and that's final. To change a discount, disable the promotion and create a new one. This keeps every past payment explainable.
* **There's no delete.** A disabled or expired promotion stays in your list.
* **The status follows the dates.** `SCHEDULED` before `starts_at`, `ACTIVE` inside the window, `EXPIRED` after `ends_at`, and `DISABLED` once you disable it. Dates are UTC.
* **All conditions must hold.** AND across condition keys, OR inside each key's `values` list. Omit a key for no condition on that dimension.
* **Fixed discounts have a currency.** A `FIXED` discount uses `discount.amounts` (one entry per currency, above zero). When you also set `conditions.amounts`, the two must list the same currencies or create returns `400`.

See [The Promotion Object](/reference/promotions/the-promotion-object) for every field.

## When more than one promotion matches

One payment gets **one** promotion. Promotions don't add up.

When two or more promotions match the same payment, Yuno picks **the one that saves the customer the most**. If two save the same amount, the oldest one (earliest `created_at`) wins. The result is the same every time you ask.

| Payment of ARS 25,000 | Saving | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promotion A: 10% off | ARS 2,500 | |
| Promotion B: ARS 3,000 off | ARS 3,000 | **Applies** |

A promotion doesn't apply when its discount would leave a total below zero. Zero-charge payments are possible when you set no minimum amount.

## What the customer sees at checkout

With the Yuno checkout you don't build anything. When the customer enters a payment method that qualifies, the checkout:

1. Asks Yuno for the quote, right after the payment method is recognized.
2. Announces the offer, for example "Your card has a 10% discount available".
3. **Updates the order summary**: the original price, the discount, and the new total. The pay button shows the new total.

If the customer changes to a payment method that doesn't qualify, the summary goes back to the regular price. When no promotion applies, the checkout looks exactly as it does today.

| Order summary | ARS |
| --- | --- |
| Original price | 25,000.00 |
| Discount (BBVA 10% off) | − 2,500.00 |
| **Total to pay** | **22,500.00** |

## Direct API integration

Without the Yuno checkout, you ask for the quote yourself, from your server, and send it on the payment:

1. **Get the quote.** Call [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote) with the payment context: account, amount, country and payment method. For a card, identify it in one of two ways: `bin` (the first 6 to 8 digits) with the card type, or the card's `token` or `vaulted_token` inside `payment_method`, and Yuno resolves the card. Never both: a BIN together with a token is rejected with `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS`, and so is a `bin` with more than 8 digits, fewer than 6, or anything but digits. Without a BIN, a promotion with a `BIN`, `BIN_RANGE` or `CARD_TYPE` condition doesn't apply.
2. **Show the price.** On `200`, show `charge_amount` to your customer. On `204`, create the payment as usual, with no `quote_id` on a discount line.
3. **Create the payment with the quote.** Send the original `amount` and `additional_data.order.discounts: [{ "quote_id": "<quote_id>" }]`, as shown below.

### Create the payment with the quote

Send the **original** amount and a discount line with `quote_id` only (up to 500 characters, no whitespace or control characters) on [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment). Yuno rejects a payment that sends the discounted amount instead of the original. A discount line carries `unit_amount` (your own discount, with `id` and `name`) **or** `quote_id` (Yuno's promotion quote), never both.

```json Request
{
    "account_id": "493e9374-510a-4201-9e09-de669d75f256",
    "country": "AR",
    "amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 25000.00 },
    "additional_data": {
        "order": {
            "discounts": [{ "quote_id": "qte_0d91f7ac3b2e" }]
        }
    }
}
```

<Warning>
**Don't subtract the discount yourself**

Send the original `amount` and the `quote_id` on a discount line. Yuno computes the charge.
</Warning>

### What the payment carries

The response and every payment webhook carry the charged amount in `amount`. Yuno fills your `quote_id` discount line (`quote_id`, `id` = the promotion id, `name`, `unit_amount` = the discount in the order currency). Your own `unit_amount` lines stay as you sent them. Next to them, a summary `promotion` object carries the quote breakdown. The provider receives the filled discount line.

```json Response
{
    "id": "b2c9d0e1-6f0e-4c2a-9c55-7d8a0b311f2e",
    "status": "SUCCEEDED",
    "amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 22500.00 },
    "additional_data": {
        "order": {
            "discounts": [
                {
                    "quote_id": "qte_0d91f7ac3b2e",
                    "id": "b7c1a4f2-6e0d-4a3b-9c55-1f2e7d8a0b31",
                    "name": "BBVA 10% off - Mother's Day",
                    "unit_amount": 2500.00
                }
            ]
        }
    },
    "promotion": {
        "quote_id": "qte_0d91f7ac3b2e",
        "promotions": [
            {
                "id": "b7c1a4f2-6e0d-4a3b-9c55-1f2e7d8a0b31",
                "name": "BBVA 10% off - Mother's Day",
                "discount_amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 2500.00 }
            }
        ],
        "original_amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 25000.00 },
        "discount_amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 2500.00 },
        "charge_amount": { "currency": "ARS", "value": 22500.00 }
    }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `promotion.quote_id` | The quote the payment was charged with. |
| `promotion.promotions[].id` | The promotion's id. |
| `promotion.promotions[].name` | The promotion name from the quote. |
| `promotion.promotions[].discount_amount` | What that promotion saved on this payment. |
| `promotion.original_amount` | The amount before the discount, the one sent on the quote. |
| `promotion.discount_amount` | Total discount (sum of the entries in `promotion.promotions`). |
| `promotion.charge_amount` | What the customer paid. Equals the payment's `amount`. |

* `original_amount − discount_amount = charge_amount`, which equals `amount`.
* Refunds and chargebacks use the charged amount, as with any payment.
* A payment without a quote has no `promotion` object (never `null`) and looks exactly as it does today.
* In webhooks, `promotion` sits next to `amount` inside the payment, with the same fields.

### The customer pays the quoted price, or the payment is rejected

A quote is valid for up to **30 minutes** on the API path, or for the checkout session on the hosted checkout. Inside that time the payment charges the quoted price, even if the promotion's end date passed after the quote was issued.

If Yuno can't honour the quote, it **rejects the payment** and never charges the full price silently. Re-quote and show the new price, or confirm the full price with the customer.

| HTTP | Code | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `422` | `PROMOTION_NOT_APPLICABLE` | The quote is validly formed but cannot apply. The body includes `reason` only (no `detail`). |
| `400` | `QUOTE_INVALID` | The quote id is malformed (over 500 characters, or with whitespace or control characters), or forged. |
| `400` | `INVALID_PARAMETERS` | A discount line has both `unit_amount` and `quote_id`, or the payment carries two quote lines. |

`PROMOTION_NOT_APPLICABLE` reasons:

| `reason` | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `QUOTE_EXPIRED` | Get a new quote. |
| `QUOTE_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` | Use the quote on the account it was made for. |
| `QUOTE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` | Send the quoted original amount. |
| `QUOTE_CONTEXT_MISMATCH` | Country, method, card type, card or session differ; get a new quote. |
| `ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE` | Yuno could not verify the quote; retry the same payment once, then get a new quote. |
| `PROMOTIONS_DISABLED` | Promotions is off for the organization; a quoted payment is rejected, never charged in full silently. |
| `SUBSCRIPTION_QUOTE_UNSUPPORTED` | Do not send a quote on a subscription payment. |

## What's not included yet

* **Subscriptions and recurring payments.** Promotions apply to one-time payments.
* **Promotion codes.** Every promotion is automatic: it applies when the conditions hold, the customer types nothing.
* **Combining promotions.** One promotion per payment.
