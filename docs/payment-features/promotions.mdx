A promotion is a discount you define once, for example "10% off with BBVA credit cards in October". Yuno checks each payment against your promotions, shows the discounted price at checkout, and charges exactly that price.

<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

Promotions is off by default. Yuno turns it on for your **organization**, and it then works on the accounts of that organization. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager to enable it, in sandbox first and then in production.

Until it's enabled, no promotion applies to your payments: [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote) answers `204`, and the checkout shows the regular price.
</Warning>

## How it works

A promotion reaches a payment in three steps. The **quote** is the link between them: it's the price the customer saw, and the payment charges that price.

1. **Create the promotion.** Use the Yuno Dashboard or [Create Promotion](/reference/promotions/create-promotion). You set the discount, the dates, and the conditions a payment must meet (card BIN or BIN range, card type, payment method, country, currency, minimum or maximum amount).
2. **Get a quote.** Before the payment, Yuno prices it: which promotion applies, the original amount, the discount, and the charge. The answer carries `quote_id` and a one-element `promotion` list. With the Yuno checkout this happens by itself. With a direct API integration, you call [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote).
3. **Create the payment with the quote.** Send `promotion.quote_id` on [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment). Yuno charges the quoted total and returns the breakdown.

<Note>
**A promotion applies only when the payment carries `promotion.quote_id`**

Yuno never discounts a payment by itself. A payment without a quote id is charged in full, exactly as today, even when a promotion would have matched it.
</Note>

## Enable promotions for your organization

1. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager to enable Promotions for your organization. Start with sandbox.
2. Once it's on, create your promotions from the Dashboard or the API. A promotion belongs to the accounts you list in `account_id` and never applies to the payments of another account.
3. Test a payment in sandbox with a card that meets the conditions, then ask for production.

Yuno can also turn Promotions off for an organization. When it's off, quotes answer `204` and the checkout shows the regular price, so the customer never sees a discount that the payment doesn't honour.

## Create a promotion

```json Create Promotion
curl --request POST \
     --url https://api-sandbox.y.uno/v1/promotions \
     --header 'X-Idempotency-Key: <Your idempotency-key>' \
     --header 'content-type: application/json' \
     --header 'private-secret-key: <Your private-secret-key>' \
     --header 'public-api-key: <Your public-api-key>' \
     --data '
{
    "account_id": ["493e9374-510a-4201-9e09-de669d75f256"],
    "name": "BBVA 10% off - Mother'\''s Day",
    "schedule": { "starts_at": "2026-10-01T03:00:00Z", "ends_at": "2026-11-01T02:59:59Z" },
    "redemption": { "mode": "AUTOMATIC" },
    "discount": { "type": "PERCENTAGE", "value": 10 },
    "conditions": {
        "bins": ["457896", "40263412"],
        "payment_methods": ["CARD"],
        "card_types": ["CREDIT"],
        "countries": ["AR"],
        "amounts": [{ "currency": "ARS", "min": 10000.00, "max": null }]
    }
}'
```

Things to know before you create one:

* **Promotions can't be edited.** The only change is to [disable](/reference/promotions/disable-promotion) one, and that's final. To change a discount, disable the promotion and create a new one. This keeps every past payment explainable.
* **There's no delete.** A disabled or expired promotion stays in your list.
* **The status follows the dates.** `SCHEDULED` before `starts_at`, `ACTIVE` inside the window, `EXPIRED` after `ends_at`, and `DISABLED` once you disable it. Dates are UTC.
* **All conditions must hold.** A promotion with no conditions applies to every payment of the account.
* **Fixed discounts have a currency.** A `FIXED` discount uses `amount[]` (one entry per currency). Amount bounds use `conditions.amounts`.

See [The Promotion Object](/reference/promotions/the-promotion-object) for every field.

## When more than one promotion matches

One payment gets **one** promotion. Promotions don't add up.

When two or more promotions match the same payment, Yuno picks **the one that saves the customer the most**. If two save the same amount, the oldest one (earliest `created_at`) wins. The result is the same every time you ask.

| Payment of ARS 25,000 | Saving | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promotion A: 10% off | ARS 2,500 | |
| Promotion B: ARS 3,000 off | ARS 3,000 | **Applies** |

A promotion doesn't apply when its discount would leave a total below zero. Zero-charge payments are possible when you set no minimum amount.

## What the customer sees at checkout

With the Yuno checkout you don't build anything. When the customer enters a payment method that qualifies, the checkout:

1. Asks Yuno for the quote, right after the payment method is recognized.
2. Announces the offer, for example "Your card has a 10% discount available".
3. **Updates the order summary**: the original price, the discount, and the new total. The pay button shows the new total.

If the customer changes to a payment method that doesn't qualify, the summary goes back to the regular price. When no promotion applies, the checkout looks exactly as it does today.

| Order summary | ARS |
| --- | --- |
| Original price | 25,000.00 |
| Discount (BBVA 10% off) | − 2,500.00 |
| **Total to pay** | **22,500.00** |

## Direct API integration

Without the Yuno checkout, you ask for the quote yourself, from your server, and send it on the payment:

1. **Get the quote.** Call [Create Promotion Quote](/reference/promotions/create-promotion-quote) with the payment context: account, amount, country, payment method and, for cards, the card type and the first 6 to 8 digits.
2. **Show the price.** On `200`, show `charge_amount` to your customer. On `204`, create the payment as usual, with no `promotion.quote_id`.
3. **Create the payment with the quote.** Send `quote_id` as `promotion.quote_id`, as shown in [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment).

Send the **original** amount on the payment and put the quote id in `promotion.quote_id`. The response and webhooks carry a `promotion` object with the three amounts; `amount` is what was charged. See [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment) and the sections below on paying with a quote.

## What's not included yet

* **Subscriptions and recurring payments.** Promotions apply to one-time payments.
* **Promotion codes.** Every promotion is automatic: it applies when the conditions hold, the customer types nothing.
* **Combining promotions.** One promotion per payment.
