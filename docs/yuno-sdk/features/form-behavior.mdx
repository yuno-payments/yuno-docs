The SDK renders one built-in form: card number, expiration date, security code, and cardholder name, plus customer and address fields when the payment method needs them. Everything on this page comes with it out of the box: no configuration, no extra code.

{/* Verified against yuno-ios master. TODO(verify): confirm Web and Android parity before publishing. */}

## Unfold the forms

Configure our unfolded forms and each method's form shows up already 
open inside the payment methods list, so shoppers enter their details 
without an extra tap. You enable it from the **Checkout Builder** in 
your [Yuno Dashboard](https://dashboard.y.uno/), no code needed.

## The cursor moves for you

Shoppers just type. As each field completes, focus jumps to the next one, with no tapping between fields. The card number is formatted and the brand detected as they type.

{/* Verified: advance triggers on brand-aware length + Luhn (card number), valid MM/YY (expiry), brand CVC length (code). Details in CardFormViewModel+Focusfields.swift. */}

## Addresses fill themselves

Cards saved on the device fill in with a tap. And when the shopper types their postal code, the SDK completes the street, city, and state for them.
