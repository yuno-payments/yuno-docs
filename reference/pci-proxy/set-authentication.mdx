keywords: ["pci proxy", "allowlist", "destination", "authentication", "hmac", "request signing", "dlocal"]

---

Registers or replaces the authentication the [forward proxy](/reference/pci-proxy/invoke-forward-proxy) applies when forwarding to this destination — a **full replace**, which is also how you rotate keys. Secrets are write-only and never returned. See the [Destination Allowlist guide](/docs/security-and-compliance/pci-proxy/allowlist#destination-authentication-request-signing) for how signing profiles work.

<Note>
**Registering credentials is sensitive**

A signing profile lets the proxy authenticate to the destination on your behalf. Restrict who can manage the allowlist; every change is recorded in the append-only audit trail.
</Note>
