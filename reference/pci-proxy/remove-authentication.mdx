---
title: "Remove Destination Authentication"
description: "Remove the signing profile from a PCI Proxy destination. The destination keeps working as a regular allowlist entry."
keywords: ["pci proxy", "allowlist", "destination", "authentication", "hmac", "request signing"]
openapi: "/openapi/pci-proxy/manage-destinations.json DELETE /pci-proxy/destinations/{id}/authentication"
---

Removes all authentication configuration from the destination. The [forward proxy](/reference/pci-proxy/invoke-forward-proxy) stops signing requests to it; the destination keeps working as a regular allowlist entry with the headers you supply yourself. The change is recorded in the append-only audit trail. See the [Destination Allowlist guide](/docs/security-and-compliance/pci-proxy/allowlist#destination-authentication-request-signing).
