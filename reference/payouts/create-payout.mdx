---
title: "Create Payout"
openapi: "/openapi/payouts/create-payout.json POST /payouts"
description: "Creates a payout to transfer funds from your account to a recipient"
---

This request allows you to create a payout to transfer funds from your account to another account or recipient.

A published payout routing for the payout method and country is required. Without one, the API answers `201` with the payout `status` set to `ERROR`.

<Note>
**Payout Methods Guide**

To see test guidelines and beneficiary information details that are needed for each integration, please refer to the Payout Methods guide.
</Note>
