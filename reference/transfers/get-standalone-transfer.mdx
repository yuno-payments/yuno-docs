---
title: "Get Standalone Transfer"
openapi: "/openapi/transfers/get-standalone-transfer.json GET /split-marketplace/transfers/{transfer_id}"
description: "Retrieves a standalone transfer with its saved direction, status, provider information, and metadata"
---

Retrieve a transfer using its unique ID. The response includes its saved `direction`: `PLATFORM_TO_RECIPIENT` or `RECIPIENT_TO_PLATFORM`.

Existing transfers return `PLATFORM_TO_RECIPIENT`. Direction is immutable after creation; reversals do not change it. A recipient-to-platform transfer remains readable if access to creating that direction is later removed.

See [Create Standalone Transfer](/reference/transfers/create-standalone-transfer) for direction defaults and availability.
