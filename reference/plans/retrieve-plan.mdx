title: "Retrieve Plan"
openapi: "/openapi/plans/retrieve-plan.json GET /subscriptions/plans/{plan_id}"
description: "Get the full details of a plan, including its phases, per-country pricing and attached promotions."
---

<Note>
**Attached promotions**

The response includes a `promotions` list with the promotions attached to the plan, each with the number of `cycles` it covers, its `on_pause` behavior, its `type` (`AUTOMATIC` or `CODE`) and its `status`. The `status` is `ENABLED` or `DISABLED`, read from the promotions engine. If the engine does not respond, the status is `UNKNOWN`. Attach and remove promotions with [Attach Promotion to Plan](/reference/plans/attach-plan-promotion) and [Remove Promotion from Plan](/reference/plans/remove-plan-promotion). See [Promotions on subscription plans](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/promotions).
</Note>
