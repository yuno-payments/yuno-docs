---
title: "Remove Promotion from Plan"
openapi: "/openapi/plans/remove-plan-promotion.json DELETE /subscriptions/plans/{plan_id}/promotions/{promotion_id}"
description: "Removes a promotion from a subscription plan. Subscriptions that already have the promotion keep their copy"
---

Removes the attachment of a promotion from a plan. The plan no longer offers the promotion, so it cannot be applied to new subscriptions on the plan. Subscriptions that already have the promotion are not changed: they keep their copy until it is `COMPLETED` or `ENDED`.

The response has no body. To end the promotion on one subscription, use [Remove Promotion from Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/remove-subscription-promotion).

<Note>
**Changing `cycles`**

Attachments are not edited. To change `cycles` or `on_pause`, remove the attachment with this endpoint and then [attach the promotion again](/reference/plans/attach-plan-promotion).
</Note>
