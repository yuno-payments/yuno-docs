---
title: "Block Onboarding"
openapi: "/openapi/recipients-for-marketplace/block-recipient.json POST /recipients/{recipient_id}/onboardings/{onboarding_id}/block"
description: "Block a recipient's onboarding to temporarily prevent payouts for compliance or risk review"
---

Blocks a recipient. This prevents the recipient from receiving payments until unblocked. While blocked, the recipient is excluded from split resolution: split-carrying refund or cancel operations that reference them fail with `INVALID_PARAMETERS`.

## Provider-level suspension

When provider-level suspension is enabled for the connection (a per-connection setting), blocking also suspends the account at the provider: an Adyen account holder is suspended, and a Stripe connected account is rejected with payouts paused. [Unblock Onboarding](/reference/recipients-for-marketplace/unblock-recipient) reactivates the account at the provider. Without the setting, blocking is Yuno-internal only and provider behavior is unchanged.

<Note>
To remove a recipient permanently instead of pausing them, use [Terminate Onboarding](/reference/recipients-for-marketplace/terminate-onboarding).
</Note>
