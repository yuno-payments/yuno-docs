---
title: "Unblock Onboarding"
openapi: "/openapi/recipients-for-marketplace/unblock-recipient.json POST /recipients/{recipient_id}/onboardings/{onboarding_id}/unblock"
description: "Unblock a previously blocked recipient onboarding to resume payouts"
---

Unblocks a recipient. This allows the recipient to receive payments again.

## Provider-level suspension

When provider-level suspension is enabled for the connection (a per-connection setting), unblocking also reactivates the account at the provider that [Block Onboarding](/reference/recipients-for-marketplace/block-recipient) suspended. If the provider declines the reactivation — for example because the suspension was initiated by the provider itself — the request returns an error identifying the provider decline and the onboarding stays `BLOCKED`. Without the setting, unblocking is Yuno-internal only.
