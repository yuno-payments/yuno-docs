---
title: "Create Recipient"
openapi: "/openapi/recipients-for-marketplace/create-recipient-1.json POST /recipients"
description: "Create a recipient to receive funds from split payments in a marketplace"
---

Creates  a new recipient for split payment scenarios. This endpoint allows merchants to define recipients that can receive portions of payments in marketplace or split payment models.

Do not send `anticipation_settings` at recipient level or inside `onboardings`, even as `null`: its presence returns `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS` before recipient creation. Omit it for ordinary creation. Configure anticipation separately through the [dedicated settings endpoints](/reference/recipients-for-marketplace/anticipation-settings) after onboarding is eligible and the provider connection is enabled.
