---
title: "Reactivate Subscription"
openapi: "/openapi/subscriptions/reactivate-subscription.json POST /subscriptions/{subscription_id}/reactivate"
description: "Returns a canceled or completed subscription to ACTIVE, keeping the same subscription."
---

Use this endpoint for win-back campaigns. The subscription returns to ACTIVE keeping its `id`, its `billing_cycles` counters, its subscription agreement, its vaulted payment method and a single continuous payment history. Nothing is duplicated, so a returning customer is not reported as a new signup.

## Which subscriptions can be reactivated

| Current `status` | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `CANCELED` | Reactivated. |
| `COMPLETED` | Reactivated. If it used every billing cycle it had, raise the limit in the same call — see below. |
| Any other status | `409`. A paused subscription is resumed with [Resume Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/resume-subscription) instead. |

## When the billing cycles are used up

A subscription that used every cycle in `billing_cycles.total` needs a larger limit before it can come back, because returning it to ACTIVE as it is would only complete it again on the first charge. Reactivating it without a new limit returns `409` and names the number it needs: `The subscription exhausted its 12 billing cycles. Send billing_cycles.total greater than 12 in the reactivate body`.

Send that number in the reactivate body and the subscription comes back in the same call. It keeps its `id`, its history and its counters, and only the limit changes. No second subscription is created.

```json
{
  "billing_cycles": {
    "total": 24
  }
}
```

The field only raises the limit:

| `billing_cycles.total` | Result |
| --- | --- |
| greater than the current total | the limit is raised and the subscription is reactivated |
| equal to or lower than the current total | `400`: `billing_cycles.total must be greater than the current total (12)` |
| sent to a subscription that has no cycle limit | `400`: `billing_cycles.total cannot be set on a subscription without a billing cycle limit` |
| omitted, or sent as `null` | the limit stays as it is, so a subscription that used it up gets the `409` above |

A subscription that still has cycles left does not need the field, but it accepts it: the reactivated subscription then runs on the longer limit.

A subscription whose `availability.finish_at` has passed is refused with `400`. Extend the window before reactivating.

## Choosing the next billing date

`billing_cycle_behavior` is the same axis used on plan changes and on resume.

Take a subscription that bills monthly on the 17th, canceled in May and reactivated on September 1:

| Value | Next charge |
| --- | --- |
| `KEEP` (default) | September 17 — the original anchor's next occurrence. |
| `RESTART` | October 17 — one full period from the reactivation date, on the same billing day. |

Omitting the field does what `KEEP` does. `KEEP` holds the original anchor: untouched while it is still ahead, and rolled to its next occurrence once it has passed. It never charges at the moment of reactivation.

That matters most on a subscriber who comes back inside a period they already paid for. Canceling does not move the billing date, so a subscription canceled on September 1 that was paid through September 17 keeps September 17 as its anchor. `KEEP` bills them on that date, the one they paid through. `RESTART` would push the charge to October 17 and hand them the rest of September and all of October for free.

## The payment method

The vaulted payment method is revalidated before the subscription is reactivated. If it is no longer enrolled, the call returns `400` and the subscription stays canceled, rather than becoming a subscription that cannot charge.

<Warning>
Revalidation confirms the payment method is still enrolled. It cannot confirm that the stored-credential mandate is still valid with the issuer, because that validity is held by the issuer and the card network, not by Yuno. A long gap between the cancellation and the reactivation raises the chance that the first charge is declined. If it is, attach a newly vaulted payment method with [Update Subscription](/reference/subscriptions/update-subscription) and retry.
</Warning>
