---
title: "Remove Promotion from Subscription"
openapi: "/openapi/subscriptions/remove-subscription-promotion.json DELETE /subscriptions/{subscription_id}/promotion"
description: "Ends the active promotion on a subscription. The next charge is the full price, and the promotion ends with the reason REMOVED"
---

Ends the active promotion on a subscription. The promotion's `status` becomes `ENDED` and its `end_reason` becomes `REMOVED`. The next charge is the full price.

To give the subscription a discount again, [apply a promotion](/reference/subscriptions/update-subscription). The new copy starts at 0 cycles used.

## Errors

| HTTP | `code` | When |
| :--- | :----- | :--- |
| `409` | `SUBSCRIPTION_PROMOTION_NOT_ACTIVE` | The subscription has no active promotion. The response includes `current_status`: the status of its latest promotion, `COMPLETED` or `ENDED`, or `null` if the subscription never had one. |

See [Subscription promotion error codes](/reference/subscriptions/subscription-promotion-error-codes) for every code.
