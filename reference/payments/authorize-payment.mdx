---
title: "Authorize Payment"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/authorize-payment.json POST /payments"
description: "Authorizes a payment amount on a card without charging the customer immediately."
---

This request allows you to authorize a payment after you created a checkout session. It just authorizes the amount (the client is not charged). To make an authorization, send `capture: false` inside the payment method detail, under the object that matches the payment method type: `payment_method.detail.card.capture` for cards and `payment_method.detail.wallet.capture` for wallets that support authorization (for example PayPal with a vaulted billing agreement). Yuno only reads the flag under the object that matches the payment method type, so `capture: false` under `card` is ignored on a wallet payment and the payment is captured right away.

<Note>
**Promotions**

To charge a promotion's price, send the original `amount` and a discount line in `additional_data.order.discounts` with `type: YUNO_PROMOTION` and the `quote_id` of a promotion quote. Yuno charges the discounted price, keeps `amount` as you sent it, and returns `discount_amount`. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions).
</Note>
