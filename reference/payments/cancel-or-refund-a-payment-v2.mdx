---
title: "Cancel or Refund a Payment (two cards)"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/cancel-or-refund-a-payment-v2.json POST /payments/{payment_id}/cancel-or-refund"
description: "Cancels or refunds a whole two-card payment, or an amount spread across both cards, without a transaction id."
---

This request cancels or refunds a payment created with [Create Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/create-payment-v2). No `transaction_id` is needed: Yuno cancels each card that is only authorized and refunds each card that was charged.

<Note>
**Partial amounts**

`amount` is optional. If you omit it, the whole payment is canceled or refunded. If you send it, Yuno spreads it across the cards, starting with the card that has the most refundable amount. An `amount` above the total refundable amount of the payment is rejected.
</Note>

The response is `201` with the payment in the v2 shape.

<Warning>
While a card has an open chargeback, refunds are rejected with `INVALID_STATUS`.
</Warning>

This request requires an `X-Idempotency-Key`. See [Authentication](/reference/getting-started/authentication#idempotency) for details.
