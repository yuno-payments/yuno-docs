---
title: "Cancel Payment (two cards)"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/cancel-payment-v2.json POST /payments/{payment_id}/transactions/{transaction_id}/cancel"
description: "Cancels the authorization of one card of a two-card payment, using that card's transaction id."
---

This request cancels one authorized card of a payment created with [Create Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/create-payment-v2). Cancel is per card: send the `id` of that card's `AUTHORIZE` transaction from `payment_method.payment_methods[].transactions`. To cancel the whole payment in one request, use [Cancel or Refund a Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/cancel-or-refund-a-payment-v2).

The response is `201` with the payment in the v2 shape.

<Warning>
While the payment is in `REFUND_RETRY_IN_PROCESS` or `CANCEL_RETRY_IN_PROCESS`, a cancel is rejected. See [Pay with two cards](/docs/payment-features/pay-with-two-cards#operations).
</Warning>

This request requires an `X-Idempotency-Key`. See [Authentication](/reference/getting-started/authentication#idempotency) for details.
