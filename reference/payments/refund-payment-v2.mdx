---
title: "Refund Payment (two cards)"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/refund-payment-v2.json POST /payments/{payment_id}/transactions/{transaction_id}/refund"
description: "Refunds one card of a two-card payment, fully or partially, using that card's transaction id."
---

This request refunds one card of a payment created with [Create Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/create-payment-v2). Refund is per card: send the `id` of that card's `PURCHASE` or `CAPTURE` transaction from `payment_method.payment_methods[].transactions`.

If you omit `amount`, the card is refunded in full. A refund on a card is capped at that card's amount minus what was already refunded on it. After one card is refunded, the payment is `SUCCEEDED / PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`; after both, it is `REFUNDED / REFUNDED`. The response is `201` with the payment in the v2 shape.

<Warning>
While a card has an open chargeback, refunds are rejected with `INVALID_STATUS`.
</Warning>

This request requires an `X-Idempotency-Key`. See [Authentication](/reference/getting-started/authentication#idempotency) for details.
