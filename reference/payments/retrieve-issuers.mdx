---
title: "Retrieve Issuers"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/retrieve-issuers.json GET /issuers"
description: "Retrieves the list of issuers that support a specific payment method."
---

This endpoint retrieves a list of all issuers that support the specific payment method. It allows you to filter issuers based on their compatibility with a particular payment method.

The API answers 201 for every `payment_method` except `PSE`, which answers 200. For example, `country_code=CO&payment_method=NEQUI` answers 201 and `country_code=CO&payment_method=PSE` answers 200.

## Banks under maintenance

When you schedule a maintenance window for a bank in the Dashboard, that bank stays in the list, moves to the end and carries `"status": "OFFLINE"`. The `status` field is optional: it is absent for every available bank and absent from the whole response when no bank is under maintenance.

Do not offer an `OFFLINE` bank to the customer. A payment created with it is declined with the response code [`BANK_UNAVAILABLE`](/reference/payments/status-and-response-codes/transaction#declined-status).

### In the Yuno SDKs

Yuno applies the window on the server, so the payment is declined with `BANK_UNAVAILABLE` on every SDK and version. You do not need to update the SDK for the window to take effect.

What changes by SDK is how the bank list looks:

- **Web SDK 2.8.0 or later**: the bank is shown as unavailable, at the end of the list, and the customer cannot choose it.
- **Android and iOS SDKs**: the bank is not marked yet. It is shown like any other bank, and the payment is declined if the customer chooses it.
