---
title: "Create Payment (two cards)"
description: "Create one payment funded by exactly two cards with POST /v2/payments, using the DIRECT, REDIRECT or SDK_CHECKOUT workflow."
openapi: "/openapi/payments/create-payment-v2.json POST /payments"
---

This request creates one payment funded by exactly two cards. Each card pays its part of `amount`, and the two parts must add up to `amount.value`. The base URL is `https://api-sandbox.y.uno/v2` in sandbox `https://api.y.uno/v2` in production (US) and `https://api.eu.y.uno/v2` in production (EMEA). Read [Pay with two cards](/docs/payment-features/pay-with-two-cards) before you integrate.

* **`DIRECT` or `REDIRECT`**: send `payment_method.payment_methods` with exactly two items, both `type: CARD`. Each item has its `amount` and `detail.card` or a `vaulted_token`.
* **`SDK_CHECKOUT`**: send `checkout.session` and the composite `payment_method.token` from the [headless Web SDK](/docs/sdks/headless-web/payment#split-one-payment-across-two-cards). Do not send `payment_methods`.

`payment_method.capture` applies to both cards and defaults to `true`. Do not send `capture` inside `detail.card`: the request is rejected.

The response has the same shape as [Retrieve Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/retrieve-payment-two-cards).

<Warning>
**All or nothing**

If one card fails, Yuno reverses the other card before it answers this request, so the response already shows the final outcome. See [All or nothing](/docs/payment-features/pay-with-two-cards#all-or-nothing).
</Warning>

<Note>
**Enablement**

In production, two-card payments are enabled per organization. Until then, this request returns `403 PRODUCT_NOT_ENABLED`. In sandbox they are enabled for every organization.
</Note>

`split_marketplace`, `external_subscription` and `amount.currency_conversion` are not allowed with two cards, and promotion quotes are not accepted.

This request requires an `X-Idempotency-Key`. See [Authentication](/reference/getting-started/authentication#idempotency) for details.
