---
title: "Retrieve Payment (two cards)"
openapi: "/openapi/payments/retrieve-payment-two-cards.json GET /payments/{payment_id}"
description: "Retrieves a payment funded by two cards, with the amount and transactions of each card, from GET /v2/payments/{payment_id}."
---

This request retrieves a payment created with [Create Payment (two cards)](/reference/payments/create-payment-v2), using the `id` in the request path. The base URL is `https://api-sandbox.y.uno/v2` in sandbox and `https://api.y.uno/v2` in production.

The response has `split_payment_methods: true`, `payment_methods_count: 2` and one entry per card in `payment_method.payment_methods`, each with its `sequence` (`1` or `2`), `type`, `amount` and `transactions`. A card has no status of its own: read each card's outcome from its transactions, and the payment outcome from the payment-level `status` and `sub_status`. See [Pay with two cards](/docs/payment-features/pay-with-two-cards#statuses).

<Note>
For a payment created with `POST /v1/payments`, use the v1 request `GET /v1/payments/{payment_id}`. The v1 response shows a payment as a whole, not each card.
</Note>
