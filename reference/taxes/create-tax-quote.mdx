---
title: "Create Tax Quote"
openapi: "/openapi/taxes/create-tax-quote.json POST /taxes/quote"
description: "Quote the tax of a Stripe Billing subscription for the customer's address and tax ID before the payment"
---

Quote the tax of the first payment of a Stripe Billing subscription, for the customer's billing address and, for a business, its tax ID. The quote uses Stripe Tax with the registrations of your Stripe account. See [Local price and taxes](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/local-price-and-taxes).

<Note>
**Enabled per organization**

Tax collection is enabled per organization and needs your Stripe Billing onboarding with Stripe Tax active. Contact your Key Account Manager (KAM) to enable it.
</Note>

- Quote again every time the country, the postal code or the tax ID changes. A quote is valid for 30 minutes.
- The postal code is required for the United States and Canada. Other countries only need the country.
- Charge `total` and send the line in the payment's `taxes` with `source: YUNO_TAX_QUOTE` and the `tax_quote_id`. After the payment is approved, Yuno creates the subscription in Stripe with the same tax on its first invoice.
- `status` is `NO_TAX` when no tax applies (`no_tax_reason` tells why, for example `REVERSE_CHARGE` for a business with a valid tax ID, or `NOT_SUBJECT_TO_TAX` where your Stripe account has no registration).
