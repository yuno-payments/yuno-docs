---
title: "Create FX Quote"
openapi: "/openapi/fx-rates/create-fx-quote.json POST /fx-rates/quote"
description: "Quote a USD price in the customer's local currency and hold it until it expires"
---

Quote a USD amount in the customer's local currency. The rate is Yuno's daily [reference rate](/reference/fx-rates/get-fx-rates) plus the markup agreed for your organization. Charge the local amount with the quote before `expires_at`. See [Local price and taxes](/docs/payment-features/subscriptions/local-price-and-taxes).

<Note>
**Enabled per organization**

FX quotes are enabled per organization. Contact your Key Account Manager (KAM) to enable them.
</Note>

- Only `USD` amounts can be quoted.
- `404 FX_QUOTE_NOT_AVAILABLE` means there is no published rate for that currency on the latest available day: charge in USD.
- An expired quote is refused at payment time. Quote again and show the customer the new amount.
