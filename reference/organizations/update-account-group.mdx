---
title: "Update Account Group"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/update-account-group.json PATCH /organizations/account-groups/{account_group_id}"
description: "Updates the name, merchant ID or parent group of an existing account group"
---

Updates the details of an existing account group: its name, its merchant ID, or the group it is nested under.

Only the fields present in the body are changed. `parent_code` has three states: omit it to leave the parent untouched, send `null` to detach the group and make it a root again, or send another group's `id` to nest the group under it. Nesting is structural only — it does not grant or revoke any permission.
