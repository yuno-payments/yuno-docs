---
title: "Delete Account Group"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/delete-account-group.json DELETE /organizations/account-groups/{account_group_id}"
description: "Deletes an empty account group"
---

Deletes an account group. The group must be empty: a group that still has accounts (`GROUP_HAS_ACCOUNTS`) or sub-groups (`GROUP_HAS_SUBGROUPS`) is rejected with `400`. Move or [remove its accounts](/reference/organizations/remove-account-from-group) and move or delete its sub-groups first. Accounts are never reassigned automatically.
