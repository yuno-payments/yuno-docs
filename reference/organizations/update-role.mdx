---
title: "Update Role"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/update-role.json PATCH /organizations/roles/{role_id}"
description: "Partially updates a custom role's permissions without changing its role type"
---

Partial update of a custom role. Note that `role_type` is immutable — an `account`-scoped role cannot be converted to `organization` scope or vice versa. Create a new role with the desired `role_type` instead. See [Create Role](/reference/organizations/create-role).
