---
title: "Remove Account from Group"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/remove-account-from-group.json DELETE /organizations/account-groups/{account_group_id}/accounts/{account_id}"
description: "Detaches an account from an account group without deleting the account"
---

<Warning>
This endpoint is not available through the public API yet. Calls return `404 NOT_FOUND` today. This page describes the intended behavior.
</Warning>

Detaches an account from an account group. The account is not deleted or disabled — it becomes a standalone account of your organization and can be attached to another group later. A group must always keep at least one account, so the last account cannot be removed this way — move it into another group or delete the account instead.
