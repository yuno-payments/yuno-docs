---
title: "Delete Account"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/delete-account.json DELETE /organizations/accounts/{account_id}"
description: "Deletes a specific merchant account within the organization"
---

Deletes an account from your organization. Deleting is permanent: the account is no longer listed or retrievable by id, and the API cannot restore it. This is not the same as a suspended account (`status: SUSPENDED`), which is only left out of listings and can still be retrieved by id.
