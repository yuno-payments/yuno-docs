---
title: "Create Role"
openapi: "/openapi/organizations/create-role.json POST /organizations/roles"
description: "Creates a custom role with a defined set of permissions for the organization"
---

Create a custom role for the organization.

Set `role_type` to scope where the role applies once assigned to a user: `account` (the default) grants access on the specific account(s) it's assigned to, while `organization` grants that same access across every account in the organization, including accounts created later.
