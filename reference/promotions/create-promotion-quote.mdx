<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

Until Yuno enables Promotions for your organization, this endpoint always answers `eligible: false`. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions#enable-promotions-for-your-organization).
</Warning>

<Note>
**Using the Yuno checkout? You don't call this endpoint**

The checkout asks for the quote by itself when the customer enters a card, and updates the price and the discount in the order summary. Call this endpoint only from a direct API integration, from your server.
</Note>

## How to use the quote

1. Send the context of the payment you're about to create. `amount` is the original amount, before any promotion.
2. If `eligible` is `true`, show `computed.total` to your customer. That's what they'll pay.
3. Create the payment with the same original `amount` and the quote in `additional_data.order.discounts`:

```json
"additional_data": { "order": { "discounts": [ { "quote_id": "qte_0d91f7ac3b2e" } ] } }
```

A promotion applies **only** when the payment carries a `quote_id`. A payment without one is charged in full.

## Rules

* **Biggest saving wins.** When two or more promotions match, the quote is for the one that saves the customer the most, then the oldest. One promotion per payment.
* **30 minutes.** A quote is valid until `quote.expires_at`. Inside that time the payment charges the quoted price, even if the promotion ended or was disabled in between.
* **Quoted price or no payment.** If the quote expired, or the payment's amount, currency, card or account differs from what you quoted, Yuno rejects the payment. It never charges the full price instead. Get a new quote and try again.
* **Nothing is consumed.** A quote is a read. Asking twice returns the same answer, and a quote you never use needs no cleanup.
* **No match isn't an error.** The answer is `200` with `eligible: false`. Create the payment as usual, with no `quote_id`.
* **`reason` says why.** `NO_MATCH`: no promotion applies to this payment. `ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE`: Yuno couldn't price the payment right now; retry, or create the payment without a `quote_id` to charge the regular price.
* **Never send a full card number.** `bin` is the first 6 to 8 digits.
