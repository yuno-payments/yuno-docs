<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

Until Yuno enables Promotions for your organization, this endpoint answers `204` (nothing applies). Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions#enable-promotions-for-your-organization).
</Warning>

<Note>
**Using the Yuno checkout? You don't call this endpoint**

The checkout asks for the quote by itself when the customer enters a card, and updates the price and the discount in the order summary. Call this endpoint only from a direct API integration, from your server.
</Note>

## How to use the quote

1. Send the context of the payment you're about to create. `amount` is the original amount, before any promotion. For a card, identify it in one of two ways: `bin` (the first 6 to 8 digits), or the card's `token` or `vaulted_token` inside `payment_method`.
2. On `200`, show `charge_amount` to your customer. That's what they'll pay. The answer includes `quote_id`, `promotions` (one entry today: `id`, `name`, `description`, `discount_amount`), and the three totals outside the list (`original_amount`, `discount_amount`, `charge_amount`).
3. Create the payment with the same original `amount` and `additional_data.order.discounts: [{ "quote_id": "<quote_id>" }]`. See [Create Payment](/reference/payments/create-payment) and [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions).

A promotion applies **only** when the payment carries that `quote_id` on a discount line. A payment without one is charged in full.

## Rules

* **Biggest saving wins.** When two or more promotions match, the quote is for the one that saves the customer the most, then the oldest. One promotion per payment; no stacking.
* **30 minutes on the API path.** A quote is valid until `expires_at` (30 minutes when there is no checkout session). Inside that time the payment charges the quoted price, even if the promotion's end date passed after the quote was issued, or you [disabled](/reference/promotions/disable-promotion) the promotion after the quote was issued.
* **Every condition must already be true.** A quote exists only when the promotion's conditions all hold for the context you send.
* **Quoted price or no payment.** If the quote expired, or the payment's amount, currency, card or account differs from what you quoted, Yuno rejects the payment with `422 PROMOTION_NOT_APPLICABLE` (or `400 QUOTE_INVALID` for a malformed id). It never charges the full price instead. Get a new quote and try again.
* **Nothing is consumed.** A quote is a read. Asking twice returns the same answer while it is valid, and a quote you never use needs no cleanup.
* **No match isn't an error.** The answer is `204` when nothing applies. Create the payment as usual, with no `quote_id` on a discount line.
* **BIN or token, never both.** A server that holds the card number sends `bin`, the first 6 to 8 digits. A server that holds a token sends `payment_method.token` or `payment_method.vaulted_token`, and Yuno resolves the BIN and the card type from the card, exactly as it does on the payment. Sending both is rejected with `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS`. Without a BIN, a promotion with a `BIN`, `BIN_RANGE` or `CARD_TYPE` condition doesn't apply.
* **Never send a full card number.** `bin` is 6 to 8 digits, digits only. More than 8 digits, fewer than 6, or anything but digits is rejected with `400 INVALID_PARAMETERS`.
