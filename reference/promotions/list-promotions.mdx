---
title: "List Promotions"
openapi: "/openapi/promotions/list-promotions.json GET /promotions"
description: "Returns the promotions of one account, paginated, with an optional status filter."
noindex: true
---

<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

This endpoint works only after Yuno enables Promotions for your organization. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions#enable-promotions-for-your-organization).
</Warning>

`account_id` is required: a list always belongs to one account. To see the promotions of several accounts, call once per account.
