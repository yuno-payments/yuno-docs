---
title: "Retrieve Promotion"
openapi: "/openapi/promotions/retrieve-promotion.json GET /promotions/{promotion_id}"
noindex: true
description: "Returns one promotion with its current status."
---

<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

This endpoint works only after Yuno enables Promotions for your organization. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions#enable-promotions-for-your-organization).
</Warning>

`status` is computed from the promotion's dates at the moment you call, so the same promotion moves from `SCHEDULED` to `ACTIVE` to `EXPIRED` with no change on your side.
