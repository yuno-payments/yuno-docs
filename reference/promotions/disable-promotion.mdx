---
title: "Disable Promotion"
openapi: "/openapi/promotions/disable-promotion.json PATCH /promotions/{promotion_id}"
description: "Stops a promotion. It's the only change a promotion accepts, and it's final."
---

<Warning>
**Promotions is enabled per organization**

This endpoint works only after Yuno enables Promotions for your organization. Ask your Technical Account Manager or Key Account Manager. See [Promotions](/docs/payment-features/promotions#enable-promotions-for-your-organization).
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Disabling is final**

A disabled promotion can't be enabled again, and there's no delete endpoint. To run the offer again, create a new promotion.
</Warning>

What disabling does and doesn't do:

* It stops **new quotes** from that moment.
* A quote issued before you disabled the promotion is still honoured until it expires (30 minutes at most). The customer already saw that price.
* Payments already charged, and their `promotion` object, don't change.

The body accepts `status` only. Any other field returns `400 INVALID_REQUEST`.
