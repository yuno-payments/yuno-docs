---
title: "Contact"
description: "How to reach Yuno for support, sales, or technical questions."
---

**Support** — Email [support@y.uno](mailto:support@y.uno) for help with an integration, your account, or an active incident.

**Sales** — [Book a demo](https://www.y.uno/en/book-a-demo) to talk with the Yuno team about a new integration.

**Dashboard** — Manage your account, credentials, and connections at [dashboard.y.uno](https://dashboard.y.uno).

**Status** — Check current platform status and incident history at [status.y.uno](https://status.y.uno).

**Documentation** — For technical questions, search this site, read the [API reference](/reference/getting-started/api-reference-overview), or connect an AI coding agent through the [docs MCP server](/setup-mcp).
